<Info>
  When a customer starts a conversation by tapping a **Click-to-WhatsApp ad** or a **Facebook/Instagram Page button**, Meta grants a longer free messaging window than the standard 24-hour service window — Gallabox calls this the **Ad Engagement Window**.
</Info>

## How it's different from the standard window

The [standard 24-hour service window](/pricing-and-billing/message-credits) **rolls forward** — every new message from the customer resets the clock to another 24 hours.

The Ad Engagement Window works differently:

- It's granted once, when the customer first messages you from the ad or Page button.
- It runs for **up to 72 hours** in total (an initial 24-hour window, extended by an additional 48 hours that Meta grants specifically for these ad-originated conversations).
- It's **static, not rolling** — it does not extend further no matter how many times the customer replies within it. Once the 72 hours are up, it's over, even mid-conversation.

Gallabox reflects the window status that Meta reports for the conversation — the countdown you see follows Meta's own timing.

## What's free during the window

Any message you send — free-form text or any template category — is free for the duration of the Ad Engagement Window.

## After it expires

Once the Ad Engagement Window closes, standard pricing rules apply again: you're back to the regular 24-hour service window and [category-based template pricing](/pricing-and-billing/message-credits).
