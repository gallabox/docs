## What are Channels?

Channels are the different ways customers can reach you through Gallabox. Each channel has its own setup process, capabilities, and pricing implications.

Gallabox currently supports three channels:

| Channel       | What it is                             | Best for                                          |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **WhatsApp**  | Full WhatsApp Business API integration | Sales, support, transactions, broadcasts          |
| **Instagram** | Instagram DM + comment automation      | Social-first brands, influencer marketing         |
| **Web Chat**  | Embedded chat widget for your website  | Website visitors, lead capture, real-time support |

WhatsApp and Instagram conversations route to the shared Inbox. Web Chat is handled in the Web Agent's chat logs and appears in the Inbox only after a WhatsApp handoff.

## Example

**Scenario:** You run a fitness studio and want to capture leads from your website, hand qualified visitors off to WhatsApp, and handle Instagram DMs from your followers.

Here's how you'd set it up:

<Steps>

<Step>
Go to **AI Agents and Bots → Create New → Chat AI Agent**, select **Web** as the channel, and configure the widget in **Settings → Web Widget Settings**. Set the welcome message to: _"Hi! Interested in our fitness classes? Chat with us or leave your number and we'll call you back."_
</Step>

<Step>
Go to **Channels → WhatsApp** and connect your WhatsApp Business account.
</Step>

<Step>
Go to **Channels → Instagram** and connect your Instagram Professional account (Business or Creator) directly.
</Step>

<Step>
Create a Chat AI Agent and select all three channels. Set its goal to qualify leads and hand off high-intent contacts to your sales team on WhatsApp.
</Step>

<Step>
Instagram and WhatsApp conversations appear in **Inbox** with channel indicators. Web Chat conversations remain in the Web Agent's chat logs unless the visitor continues through a WhatsApp handoff.
</Step>

</Steps>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Can I connect multiple WhatsApp numbers?">
Yes. You can connect up to your plan's channel limit. Each WhatsApp number appears as a separate connection in the Channels section. Additional channels can be added as add-ons on Essential and Advanced plans.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can one Chat AI Agent handle all three channels?">
Yes. When creating a Chat AI Agent, you can select multiple channels. The agent will handle conversations on all selected channels. Note that the agent's behaviour differs per channel — see [Chat AI Agent Channel Behaviour](/ai-agents-and-bots/chat-ai-agent/channel-behaviour).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What's the 24-hour messaging window?">
A Meta policy limiting free-form messages to within 24 hours of the user's last message; it applies to WhatsApp (templates can be sent after) and Instagram (nothing can be sent after), but not Web Chat. See [Instagram DM Conversations](/instagram/instagram-dm-conversations#the-24-hour-messaging-window).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can customers switch between channels mid-conversation?">
Not automatically. Each conversation is tied to one channel. However, you can handoff a Web Chat conversation to WhatsApp using the handoff feature in the Chat AI Agent settings — useful when a website visitor wants to continue the conversation on WhatsApp.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My WhatsApp connection is stuck at 'Pending verification' — what do I do?">
Meta's WhatsApp Business API verification can take 24–48 hours for new businesses. If your connection is stuck:

1. **Check your Meta Business Manager** — go to business.facebook.com and check if verification is pending or rejected there.
2. **Verify your business documents** — Meta requires business registration documents (GSTIN in India, company registration elsewhere). Upload them in Meta Business Manager.
3. **Check your phone number** — the WhatsApp number must be on the same account as your Meta Business verification.
4. **Wait 48 hours** — if verification is in progress, it typically completes within 24–48 hours.
5. If rejected, Meta will show a reason. Fix the issue and resubmit.

If the verification expires or fails repeatedly, [contact Gallabox support](mailto:support@gallabox.com) with your account details.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
