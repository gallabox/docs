Gallabox billing has three parts, and they work differently on purpose:

|  | **Plan** | **Message Credits** | **AI Credits** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | A flat recurring subscription fee | A usage-based add-on | A usage-based add-on |
| What it buys | Unlocks Gallabox features — bot nodes, integrations, seats, and your monthly AI credit allowance | Pays for WhatsApp's per-message template charges | Pays for AI-powered feature usage (Chat AI Agent, GPT nodes, AI Reply, and more) |
| Billing shape | Fixed — billed monthly or annually | Prepaid wallet, consumed per message sent | Monthly allowance included in your plan, topped up as you use more |

Your **plan** decides what you _can_ do. **Message Credits** and **AI Credits** are the two usage-based meters that run independently on top of it — topping up one never affects the other, and neither is required to keep your subscription active.
