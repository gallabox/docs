The Omnichannel Inbox brings WhatsApp and Instagram conversations into one shared workspace. Web Chat conversations appear in the Inbox only after a WhatsApp handoff.

## Plan Availability

Available on all Gallabox plans.

## What is the Omnichannel Inbox?

The Omnichannel Inbox is the shared workspace where your team handles WhatsApp and Instagram conversations. Each conversation has a channel indicator, so you can identify its source without switching apps.

Web Chat works differently. A Web Chat conversation without a handoff remains in the Web Agent's chat logs. If the visitor continues through a WhatsApp handoff, the earlier Web Chat history becomes visible in the resulting WhatsApp conversation.

## How Conversations Arrive in the Inbox

| Channel       | How it appears                                                     |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **WhatsApp**  | Incoming messages from connected WhatsApp Business accounts        |
| **Instagram** | DMs from connected Instagram accounts + comment automation replies |
| **Web Chat**  | Only after the visitor continues through a WhatsApp handoff         |

Each conversation shows:

* **Channel icon** — WhatsApp or Instagram
* **Contact name** — from your contacts database or the channel profile
* **Last message preview** — the most recent message in the conversation
* **Timestamp** — when the last message was received
* **Status** — Open or Closed. (Unassigned is not a status — it means an open conversation has no owner yet.)

## Channel Indicators

Every conversation in the inbox has a channel indicator so your team always knows the source:

| Channel   | Icon             | Colour                   |
| --------- | ---------------- | ------------------------ |
| WhatsApp  | WhatsApp icon    | Green                    |
| Instagram | Instagram icon   | Gradient (orange/purple) |

You can filter the inbox by channel to focus on one channel at a time. For example, during a social media campaign, you might filter to Instagram only to handle comment-driven conversations.

## How to Use the Inbox

### Open a Conversation

1. Go to **Inbox** in the left navigation of Gallabox.
2. Click on any conversation in the inbox.
3. The conversation opens in the right panel — with full message history.
4. Type your reply in the compose box and click **Send**.

### Filter by Channel

1. Go to **Inbox** in the left navigation.
2. Click the **filter icon** above the conversation list.
3. Select **Channel** → choose WhatsApp or Instagram.
4. The inbox now shows only conversations from the selected channel.

### Assign a Conversation

1. Open a conversation.
2. Click the **Assign** button in the top bar.
3. Select a team member or team from the list.
4. The conversation is assigned and the assignee is notified.

### Resolve a Conversation

1. Open a conversation.
2. Click **Resolve** in the top bar.
3. The conversation moves to the Closed list. It can be reopened with **Reopen** at any time.

If the conversation is unassigned when you resolve it, Gallabox assigns it to you.

## Example

**Scenario:** You run a 5-person customer support team. You want WhatsApp conversations assigned to your sales team and Instagram conversations assigned to your social media team.

Here's how you'd set it up:

<Steps>

<Step title="Create a view">
Go to **Inbox → Views** and create a view called "Unassigned Instagram" filtered to the Instagram channel and unassigned conversations.
</Step>

<Step title="Create assignment rules">
Assignment Rules live in each channel's settings — go to **Channels → \[WhatsApp/Instagram] → Settings → Assignment Rules** and create rules:

* Rule 1: If channel = WhatsApp → assign to Sales team
* Rule 2: If channel = Instagram → assign to Social team
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm the routing">
Now WhatsApp conversations auto-assign to Sales and Instagram conversations auto-assign to Social.
</Step>

</Steps>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Can I see which channel a conversation came from?">
Yes. Every conversation in the Inbox has a channel icon next to the contact name. You can also filter the Inbox by channel to focus on WhatsApp or Instagram conversations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I have different inboxes for different teams?">
The Inbox is not a separate object you create. It is a filtered view of conversations. To give each team its own workspace, create team-specific Views and use the Assignment Rules in each channel's settings to route conversations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens when a conversation is assigned to a Chat AI Agent?">
When a conversation is assigned to a Chat AI Agent, the agent responds based on its role, goal, and instructions. The agent continues until it completes the task, hands off to a human agent, or reaches its configurable inactivity timeout. The default timeout for WhatsApp and Instagram is 3 minutes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I move a conversation from one channel to another?">
Not automatically. Each conversation is tied to the channel where it started. A Web Chat visitor can continue through a WhatsApp handoff using a pre-filled message. The earlier Web Chat history then becomes visible in the resulting WhatsApp conversation.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
