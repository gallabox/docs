> **Who can use this?**
>
> * **Import and export:** Owners and Admins, and custom roles with the **Can manage contact list** permission. Export also needs permission to see personal information (phone numbers and emails).
> * **Merge:** anyone whose role can edit contacts.
> * **Delete:** Owners, and custom roles with the **Can manage contact list** permission. The default **Admin** role can't delete contacts.

Your customer database inside Gallabox — every person who has messaged you or been imported, with tags, attributes, and segments for targeting.

## In this section

* [Segments](/contacts/segments) — group contacts by attributes and behaviour for targeted broadcasts
* [Block and opt-out](/contacts/block-and-opt-out) — block a number, or let customers opt out of marketing messages

## Import contacts

Import adds contacts from a spreadsheet straight into **Contacts**. You don't need to create a broadcast to do it.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the import">
Go to **Contacts**, click **Import**, then click **Import file**. Clear any filters first — the **Import** button is hidden while filters are applied.
</Step>

<Step title="Download the sample">
Download the sample file and add your contacts to it. Remove any columns you don't want to import.
</Step>

<Step title="Upload your file">
Upload the file, then fill in:

* **Import Name** — a name to find this import later.
* **Import Type** — **Add new contacts** or **Add new & update existing contact**. See [How duplicates are handled](#how-duplicates-are-handled).
* **Marketing Opt-In** — **Yes** or **No**. This applies only to new contacts. Existing contacts keep their current opt-in.
* **Add Contact Tags** (optional) — tags added to every contact in this file.

Click **Next**.
</Step>

<Step title="Map fields">
Match each column in your file to a Gallabox contact field, or create a new field. Click **Confirm** to start the import.
</Step>

</Steps>

The import runs in the background. To check progress or download the error report, go to **Contacts → Import → Import history**.

### File requirements

* **Format:** Excel (`.xlsx`) only. If your data is in a CSV file, open it in Excel or Google Sheets and save it as `.xlsx` first.
* **Size:** under 15 MB, up to 100,000 contacts per file.
* **Phone numbers:** include the country code. Numbers without one get your account's country code.
* If a phone number shows as `8.5265E+10`, format the cell as a number with zero decimal places before you upload.

### How duplicates are handled

Gallabox treats a row as an existing contact when its phone number or email matches a contact you already have.

| Import Type | Rows that match an existing contact | Rows that don't match |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Add new contacts** | Skipped and listed in the error report as "Contact already exists" | Created |
| **Add new & update existing contact** | The existing contact is updated with the mapped values | Created |

If a row matches more than one existing contact, it's skipped and listed in the error report.

## Export contacts

1. Go to **Contacts**.
2. Apply filters if you only want some contacts. With no filters, all contacts are exported.
3. Click **Export**, then **Export contacts**.
4. Click **Confirm**.

The file is emailed to the address on your Gallabox login. You can track the export under **Export → Active exports**.

If you don't see **Export**, your role can't export contacts or can't see personal information. Ask your account Owner to review your role.

## Merge contacts

Merging joins two records of the same person that came in on different channels — for example, a WhatsApp contact and an Instagram contact. The secondary contact's conversations and messages move to the primary contact. The secondary contact is then deleted.

1. Go to **Contacts**.
2. On the contact you want to keep, click the **⋮** menu, then **Merge Contact**.
3. Under **Secondary contact**, search for and select the other record.
4. Click **Merge**.

**Limits:**

* You can't merge two contacts that both have a WhatsApp number, both have an Instagram account, or both have an email. Merging only combines different channels.
* **Merge Contact** doesn't appear for a contact that already has WhatsApp, Instagram, and email, or for a WhatsApp contact that has only a [BSUID](/whatsapp/customer-identity-bsuid) and no phone number.

To clean up two WhatsApp contacts for the same person, keep the one you want and [delete](#delete-contacts) the other.

## Delete contacts

Deleting a contact can't be undone.

**Delete one contact:**

1. Go to **Contacts**.
2. Click the **⋮** menu on the contact, then **Delete**.
3. Confirm.

**Delete many contacts at once:**

1. Go to **Contacts**.
2. Select the contacts using the checkboxes. To delete every contact that matches your filters, apply the filters, select a contact, then click **Select [number] contacts that match this Filters**.
3. Click **Delete**, then confirm.

You can delete up to 50,000 contacts in one go. Large deletions run in the background, so contacts may take a few minutes to disappear.

If **Delete** is greyed out or missing, your role can't delete contacts. Only Owners and custom roles with **Can manage contact list** can. Ask your account Owner to delete them, or to give you that permission in [Users, teams and roles](/settings/users-teams-and-roles).

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Can I import contacts without sending a broadcast?">
Yes. Use **Contacts → Import → Import file**. Uploading contacts while you create a broadcast is a separate option for that broadcast.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why is Delete disabled for an Admin?">
The default **Admin** role can view, add, edit, import, and export contacts, but not delete them. Deleting needs the Owner role or a custom role with **Can manage contact list**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I merge two WhatsApp contacts?">
No. Merge only combines contacts from different channels. Delete the duplicate instead.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
