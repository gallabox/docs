title: "Getting Started"
---

Gallabox brings customer conversations, messaging channels, automation, and reporting into one workspace. Start by connecting a channel, then use the product sections below to set up the workflows your team needs.

## Start Here

* [Connect Your WhatsApp](/whatsapp/connect-your-whatsapp) to add your WhatsApp Business number.
* [Connect Instagram Channel](/instagram/connect-instagram-channel) to add a professional Instagram account.
* [Compare Channels](/concepts/channels) to understand how WhatsApp, Instagram, and Web differ.
* [Omnichannel Inbox](/conversations/omnichannel-inbox) to begin handling customer conversations.

## Explore by Product Area

* [Inbox](/conversations/overview) for team conversations, views, productivity tools, and AI Reply.
* [Contacts](/contacts/overview) for customer records and segments.
* [AI Agents & Bots](/ai-agents-and-bots/overview) for rule-based automation and Chat AI Agents.
* [WhatsApp](/whatsapp/overview), [Instagram](/instagram/overview), and [Web](/web-chat/overview) for channel-specific setup and features.
* [Integrations](/integrations/overview) and [Analytics](/reports-and-analytics/overview) for connected systems and reporting.

Account configuration remains under [Settings](/settings/overview). Plan, credit, and data-protection references remain under [Pricing & Billing](/pricing-and-billing/overview) and [Privacy & Security](/privacy-and-security/overview).
