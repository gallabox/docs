> **Who can use this?**
>
> * Gallabox users with **channel management permissions**.
> * Roles: Owners, Admins, or a role with channel management permission.

<Info>

Connect Instagram to receive and reply to DMs in your Gallabox Inbox, and to automate comment replies. You log in with Instagram directly. A Facebook Page is not required.

</Info>

## Prerequisites

You need an **Instagram Professional account** (Business or Creator) and its login details. Personal accounts can't be connected. To switch, go to **Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account** in the Instagram app.

## How to Connect

<Tabs>

<Tab title="New to Gallabox">

1. On **Where would you like to start?**, choose **Instagram**.
2. Click **Connect Instagram**, log in to Instagram, and allow all permissions.
3. Check the business details Gallabox fills in from your profile, and tell us how you plan to use Gallabox. Click **Submit**.
4. On **Congratulations!**, click **Get Started**.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Existing Gallabox account">

1. Go to **Instagram → Channel settings**. If you already have an Instagram channel, click **Add**.
2. Click **Connect Instagram**, log in to Instagram, and allow all permissions.
3. On Essential and Advanced, Gallabox creates an AI DM agent from your website. This takes about 2–3 minutes. When it's ready, click **Go to inbox**.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

If you see **The maximum number of channels has been reached**, click **Upgrade** to add more accounts.

## After connecting

* **Route new DMs:** In **Instagram → Channel settings → Configuration**, open **Assignment Rules** and choose a team or teammate.
* **Automate comments:** Go to **Instagram → Posts**. See [Comment Automation](/instagram/comment-automation).
* **Add an AI DM agent:** Create a Chat AI Agent with Medium set to **Instagram**, publish it, and assign it to this channel. It replies to DMs but can't send the first message.

## Refresh permissions

If the channel page shows **Your channel token has expired.**, go to **Instagram → Channel settings → Configuration** and click **Refresh permissions**. Log in with the **same** Instagram account and allow all permissions.

## Delete the Instagram channel

<Warning>

Deleting the channel permanently removes its conversations, data, DM agents, and comment automations. This can't be undone.

</Warning>

1. Go to **Instagram → Channel settings → Configuration** and click **Delete Channel**.
2. Enter the code sent to your email and click **Verify**.
3. Select a reason, type `CONFIRM`, and click **Delete**.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="I can't connect my Instagram account — what do I check?">
1. It must be a Professional account, not a personal one.
2. Allow every permission when you log in. If you skipped one, connect again.
3. If you see "already exists", the account is already connected here. Delete the old channel first.
4. Check that you haven't reached your plan's limit.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I connected Instagram but I'm not receiving DMs — what do I check?">
1. If the channel page says your token has expired, click [Refresh permissions](#refresh-permissions).
2. Make sure the customer messaged you first. You can't start a conversation on Instagram.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I send proactive messages on Instagram?">
No. Instagram doesn't let businesses start a conversation. You can reply within 24 hours of someone's last message. See [the 24-hour messaging window](/instagram/instagram-dm-conversations#the-24-hour-messaging-window).

To reach people who haven't messaged you, use [Comment Automation](/instagram/comment-automation). When someone comments on your post, it can send them a DM.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I connect multiple Instagram accounts?">
Yes. Each plan includes one. Add up to 3 more on Essential, or up to 10 on Advanced, with channel add-ons.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Analytics show my Instagram data as 'Others' — why?">
Instagram traffic may show as "Others" in the dashboard. Messaging isn't affected. Use Instagram Insights to track Instagram performance.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
