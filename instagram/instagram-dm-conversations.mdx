## What are Instagram DM Conversations?

When someone sends you a Direct Message on Instagram, it appears in your Gallabox Inbox alongside your WhatsApp conversations. You can reply without switching to Instagram.

Instagram conversations in Gallabox include:

* **Direct Messages (DMs)** — private messages sent to your Instagram Business account
* **Story replies** — replies to your Instagram stories
* **Reels engagement** — messages from Reels interactions

## How to Use Instagram DM Conversations

1. Go to **Channels → Instagram** in the left navigation of Gallabox.
2. Verify your Instagram account is connected and the status shows **Connected**.
3. Open any conversation with an **Instagram icon** in the inbox.
4. Type your reply in the compose box and click **Send**.
5. The customer receives your reply in their Instagram DMs.

## The 24-Hour Messaging Window

**What it means:** Meta's business policy allows businesses to send free-form messages to users only within 24 hours of the user's last message. After that window closes, you cannot send outbound messages on Instagram — the customer must message you again.

### How it works in practice

| Scenario                               | What you can send                             |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Within 24hr of customer's last message | Any message (free-form text, images, etc.)    |
| After 24hr window closes               | Nothing — the customer must message you again |

### What happens if you try to send outside the window

If you try to send a free-form message after the 24-hour window has closed, Gallabox will show an error:

> "Message rejected. The 24-hour messaging window has expired."

After the window closes, the conversation can only continue when the customer messages you again.

### Extending the window

The 24-hour window resets when the customer sends you another message.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Why can't I send a message to an Instagram contact?">
The 24-hour messaging window has likely expired. Meta only allows free-form messages within 24 hours of a customer's last message. After the window closes, you cannot send any outbound message on Instagram — Instagram has no template message mechanism. The customer must message you again to reopen the window.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Instagram DMs are not appearing in my inbox">
Check these things in order:

1. Is your Instagram account connected? Go to **Channels → Instagram** and verify the connection status shows **Connected**.
2. Is it an Instagram Professional account (Business or Creator)? Personal accounts cannot be connected.
3. Has the customer sent you a DM first? Gallabox only receives messages from your connected Instagram account — if the customer hasn't messaged you yet, you can't initiate contact outside the 24-hour window.
4. Did you grant all requested permissions during the direct Instagram connection? Disconnect and reconnect the account if permissions were skipped.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My Instagram account shows as connected but I'm not receiving messages">
Check these in order:

1. **Is your Instagram account a Professional account?** Go to Instagram Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account (Business or Creator).
2. **Has the customer sent you a DM first?** Gallabox only receives messages from contacts who have messaged your account. If a customer hasn't messaged you yet, you cannot initiate contact outside the 24-hour window.
3. **Did you grant the requested permissions?** If messaging permissions were skipped during the direct Instagram connection, disconnect and reconnect the account.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
