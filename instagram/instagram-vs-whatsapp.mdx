## What are the Key Differences?

WhatsApp and Instagram are both powerful customer communication channels, but they serve different purposes and support different features. Understanding these differences helps you decide which channel to prioritise — or whether to use both.

## How to Use Each Channel

### When to Prioritise WhatsApp

WhatsApp is the right primary channel if:

1. You need **broadcast campaigns** — send promotions, updates, and reminders to thousands of contacts at once.
2. You want **in-chat payments** — WhatsApp Pay and UPI support native payments.
3. You're running **e-commerce** — product catalog, shop, WhatsApp Flows, and cart abandonment recovery are all WhatsApp-only.
4. You need **structured bot flows** — step-by-step flows with conditions, ask-questions, and action nodes are not available on Instagram.
5. Your customers are **already on WhatsApp** — WhatsApp has the largest user base in most markets.

### When to Prioritise Instagram

Instagram is the right primary channel if:

1. Your brand is **social-first** — content marketing, influencer partnerships, visual products.
2. You want to **engage post commenters** — comment automation lets you auto-reply to comments and start conversations.
3. Your audience is **younger or discovery-focused** — Instagram is where customers discover brands through content.
4. You want to **drive traffic to WhatsApp** — use Instagram to attract followers, then route high-intent contacts to WhatsApp for transactions.

### When to Use Both

Use both channels when:

* You have a **social commerce strategy** — Instagram for discovery and engagement, WhatsApp for sales and support.
* You want to **capture leads from multiple touchpoints** — website visitors via Web Chat, social followers via Instagram, and existing customers via WhatsApp.
* You need a **full-funnel approach** — attract on Instagram, qualify on WhatsApp, convert via broadcasts.

## Example

**Scenario:** You run a beauty brand with 50k Instagram followers and want to convert social engagement into WhatsApp customers.

Here's how you'd use both channels:

<Steps>

<Step title="Instagram">
Post content regularly and enable comment automation. When followers comment on posts, the Chat AI Agent replies with a helpful response and offers a WhatsApp link for exclusive offers.
</Step>

<Step title="WhatsApp">
Once followers are on WhatsApp, add them to a broadcast segment. Send weekly product updates, exclusive discount codes, and abandoned cart reminders.
</Step>

<Step title="Automation">
Create a WhatsApp sequence for new WhatsApp contacts: Day 1: Welcome + discount code. Day 3: Best-seller showcase. Day 7: Limited-time offer.
</Step>

<Step title="Analytics">
Track Instagram comment engagement vs WhatsApp broadcast conversion rates to understand which channel drives more revenue.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Check>

**Pro tip:** Use Instagram comment automation as a lead generation tool, not just a support tool. Set the agent's goal to offer something valuable (e.g., early access, discount, free sample) in exchange for the contact sharing their WhatsApp number. This builds your WhatsApp audience from your Instagram following.

</Check>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Can I use bot flows on Instagram?">
No. Rule-based bot flows are available only for WhatsApp. For Instagram automation, use a Chat AI Agent or Comment Automation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which channel should I connect first?">
If you're starting from scratch, connect WhatsApp first — it has the most features (broadcasts, payments, bot flows, templates). Then add Instagram to capture social-first customers and drive them to WhatsApp for transactions. Web Chat is a good addition if you have a website and want to capture leads.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can my Chat AI Agent handle both WhatsApp and Instagram?">
Yes. When creating a Chat AI Agent, you can select multiple channels. The agent will handle conversations on all selected channels. Note that the agent can't use bot flows on Instagram — it can only have autonomous AI conversations and use system actions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How are WhatsApp and Instagram connections different?">
WhatsApp and Instagram use separate Meta APIs and are connected independently. WhatsApp uses a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA). Instagram connects directly through an Instagram Professional account (Business or Creator) and does not require a Facebook Page.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
