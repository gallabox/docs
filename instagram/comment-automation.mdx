> **Who can use this?**
>
> * Gallabox users with the **Instagram channel connected**.
> * Roles: Owners, Admins, or a role with permission to edit the Instagram channel.

<Info>

When someone comments on your post or reel, AI replies under the comment and sends them a DM. When they reply to the DM, the conversation goes to the team, teammate, or AI DM agent you choose.

</Info>

## Plan Availability

| Capability                                       | Basic | Essential | Advanced |

| Comment Automation (comment replies and DMs)     |   ✓   |     ✓     |     ✓    |
| Custom [intents](/instagram/intents)              |   —   |     ✓     |     ✓    |
| Hand DM replies to an AI DM agent                |   —   |     ✓     |     ✓    |

AI credits from your [AI Wallet](/pricing-and-billing/ai-wallet) are charged once per comment automation. If your wallet runs out, comment replies and DMs stop until you top up.

<Note>

Gallabox detects the intent of every comment, even when automation is off, so intent reports on **Instagram → Comments** and each post's details stay complete. Intent detection can't be turned off.

</Note>

## Setting up comment automation

### Prerequisites

* An **Instagram Professional account**, [connected to Gallabox](/instagram/connect-instagram-channel).
* **AI credits** in your [AI Wallet](/pricing-and-billing/ai-wallet).

### Choose what to automate

Go to **Instagram → Posts**. Posts are tagged **Live** or **Not set up**, and a post shows up only after it gets a new comment.

| To automate | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Your first posts | Click **Automate your first post**, then select the posts you want, or **Select all posts**. |
| One post | Click **Setup automation** on the post card. |
| Posts you haven't published yet | See [Automate future posts](#automate-future-posts). |

### Set up the automation

<Steps>

<Step title="Choose when to reply">
| Trigger | Replies when |
| --- | --- |
| **Comments that match an intent** | The comment matches an [intent](/instagram/intents) you select, such as **Buying**. |
| **Smart Keyword** | The comment includes your keyword. AI may also catch close spellings of it, which improves your chances of a match. |
| **Any comment** | Always. Every comment gets a reply. |
</Step>

<Step title="Tell AI how to reply">
Describe the tone and content. For example: `Keep replies under 15 words. If someone asks about price, say we've sent the details by DM.`
</Step>

<Step title="Set up the DM automation">
1. Turn on **Send a DM after replying to the comment**.
2. Describe what the DM should say. Instagram lets you send one DM per comment. Keep it short and invite a reply.
3. Under **Assignee**, choose who takes over when the commenter replies: **Teams**, **Users**, **Agents**, or **Unassigned**.
</Step>

<Step title="Test and turn on">
Type a comment in the test pane and check the **Comment** and **DM** tabs. When the replies look right, click **Turn on automation**. Test comments never reach Instagram.
</Step>

</Steps>

### Automate future posts

After your first automation, click the **Automate your future posts** card on **Posts** and choose:

* **Next post** — runs on the next post or reel you publish, then stops.
* **All future posts** — runs on every new post until you turn it off.

Each new post copies these settings and keeps them. Later changes to the future-posts automation don't update posts already in Gallabox.

## How DMs work

* **One DM per comment.** Instagram allows a single DM in response to each comment. Make it count: keep it short and ask a question so the commenter replies.
* **DMs follow your reply trigger.** When the DM is on, every comment that meets your **Choose when to reply** trigger gets a comment reply and a DM. For example, with **Any comment**, every comment gets both. With an intent trigger, you can send DMs for only some of those intents in **DM advanced settings**.
* **Each comment is handled on its own.** If someone leaves three matching comments, they can get three DMs.
* **The conversation starts when they reply.** Their reply opens a conversation in your Inbox, assigned to your **Assignee**. You then have 24 hours to keep chatting. See [the 24-hour messaging window](/instagram/instagram-dm-conversations#the-24-hour-messaging-window).

## What gets skipped

Comment Automation replies only to new, top-level comments. It skips replies inside a thread, your own comments, comments left before you turned automation on, and contacts on your Block List. Stories and Live videos are not supported yet.

## Using Intents

Intents let you reply only to comments that matter, such as buying questions. To create, test, or manage intents, see [Intents](/instagram/intents).

## Managing automated replies

Go to **Instagram → Comments** to see each comment, its intent, and the status of its reply and DM. Click a **Skipped** or **Failed** status to see why.

To change an automation, click **Edit automation** on the post or the future-posts card, then click **Publish changes**.

### Disabling automation per post

Open the post, click **Edit automation**, then click **Turn off**.

### Pausing all comment automation

There is no single switch. Turn off the future-posts automation, then turn off each post tagged **Live**. Your settings and Instagram connection are kept, and comment intents are still detected for reporting.

## Why a comment didn't get a reply

| Reason shown | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Automation is off for this post** | Set up automation on the post. |
| **Intent not in the reply filter** / **No keyword matched** | Add the intent or keyword to your trigger, or use **Any comment**. |
| **Spam comment** / **Uncategorized comment** | Use **Smart Keyword** or **Any comment** to reply to these. |
| **Contact is blocked on this channel** | Unblock them in **Channel settings → Block List**. |
| **Insufficient AI credits** | Top up your [AI Wallet](/pricing-and-billing/ai-wallet). |
| **DM is off** / **Intent not in the DM filter** | Turn on the DM, or add the intent in **DM advanced settings**. |

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="An automated reply was wrong — how do I fix it?">
Click **Edit automation** on the post, make your instructions more specific, test again, and click **Publish changes**. Delete the wrong reply in the Instagram app if needed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I set a fixed reply instead of AI?">
No. Replies and DMs are always written by AI. To control the wording, add rules to your instructions, such as "Always end with: Check your DMs!"
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I changed my future-posts automation but older posts still reply the old way — why?">
Each post keeps the settings it had when it arrived. Click **Edit automation** on each older post to update it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where did Simulated mode go?">
It's replaced by the test pane in the editor. Nothing reaches Instagram until you click **Turn on automation**.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
