> **Who can use this?**
>
> * Owners and Admins, and roles that can update AI agents.

Use analytics when you want to know how many chats the agent handled, how many finished, and how many went silent.

This lives on the agent, not under the main Analytics section. Path: **AI Agents and Bots → [your agent] → Analyze**.

## Plan Availability

| Feature                                             | Basic | Essential | Advanced |
| --------------------------------------------------- | :---: | :-------: | :------: |
| Total, Ended, and Dropped sessions                    |  Yes  |    Yes    |   Yes    |
| Successful sessions                                  |  No   |    No     |   Yes    |

## What you see

| Metric                  | Meaning |
| ----------------------- | ------- |
| **Total sessions**      | How many sessions the agent handled in the date range |
| **Ended sessions**      | Sessions that reached a normal end |
| **Dropped sessions**   | Sessions where the customer stopped before the chat finished |
| **Successful sessions** | Sessions that met the success criteria you set (Advanced only) |

Below the cards is a sessions table. Click a row for the contact, status (including drop reason), channel, session ID, credits used, and model. **Jump to conversation** is there for WhatsApp and Instagram, not Web Chat.

The drawer has **Chat Log** and **Analyze**. Analyze shows an AI summary of the outcome. Outcome scoring depends on your plan.

## How to open it

1. Go to **AI Agents and Bots**.
2. Open the agent.
3. Click **Analyze**.

## How to use the numbers

### Dropped sessions

A high dropped count means people left mid-chat. Typical causes:

* The first reply is off-topic
* The agent asks too much before it helps
* The answer is not in the Knowledge Base

Open a few dropped sessions and find the last message before silence. That is the spot to fix.

### Successful sessions (Advanced)

This measures whether the agent actually did the job, not just whether the chat ended. If Ended is high and Successful is low, the agent is closing chats without resolving them.

Review ended-but-unsuccessful sessions and look for a pattern in the last few replies.

<Check>

Track Ended and Dropped week over week. If Dropped jumps after a Knowledge Base update, the new files may be confusing the agent. Roll that content back and check what changed.

</Check>

## Example

You have a Web Chat support agent. Dropped sessions look high.

1. Go to **AI Agents and Bots → Support Agent → Analyze**.
2. Set the date range to Last 30 days.
3. You see 420 Total, 280 Ended, 140 Dropped. About a third of people left.
4. Open several **Dropped** rows.
5. People go silent right after the agent asks for an email with no reason.
6. On **Home**, update Instructions: _"Before you ask for email, say you need it to look up their account."_
7. Publish, then watch Dropped for two weeks.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Where is AI Agent Analytics? I don't see it under Analytics.">
It is the **Analyze** tab on the agent, not the main Analytics section.

1. Go to **AI Agents and Bots**.
2. Open your agent.
3. Click **Analyze**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What's the difference between Ended and Dropped?">
**Ended** reached a normal finish, even if the issue was not fully solved.

**Dropped** means the customer stopped before that.

High Dropped usually means a friction point: a confusing question, or an answer the Knowledge Base cannot support.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why is Successful sessions locked?">
It needs the Advanced plan. On Basic and Essential the card is locked. [Upgrade](/pricing-and-billing/billing-and-subscriptions) to unlock it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I export session data?">
Yes. On Analyze, open **Export** next to the search box. Choose **Export as Excel**, or **History** for past exports.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
