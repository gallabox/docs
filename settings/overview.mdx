Configure your Gallabox workspace — team-wide content tools, developer settings, and security.

## In this section

* [Tags](/settings/tags) — label contacts and conversations for filtering and segmentation
* [Variables](/settings/variables) — insert dynamic values like names and order details into messages
* [Webhooks](/settings/webhooks) — push Gallabox events to your own systems in real time
* [Account Security](/settings/security) — overview of the account security features available in your workspace
* [Two Factor Authentication](/settings/two-factor-authentication) — add a second login factor beyond your password
* [Activity Log](/settings/activity-log) — track who did what, and when, across your workspace
