title: "Country calling codes"
description: "Which rate card row a phone number bills at, by its country calling code"
---

Every WhatsApp message is charged at the rate for **your customer's country**, read from the country calling code at the start of their phone number. This page maps each calling code to the row you read on the [WhatsApp Rate Card](/pricing-and-billing/whatsapp-pricing/rate-card).

<Info>
  **How to use this page.** Take your customer's number in international format — say `+971 50 123 4567`. Strip the `+`, match the longest calling code at the start (`971`), and look it up below: United Arab Emirates, which is its own row on the rate card. Then read that row on your billing currency's tab.
</Info>

Countries **not listed** on this page bill at the **Other** row at the bottom of each rate card tab.

A **\*** after a market name means that country becomes its own rate card row on **October 1, 2026**. Until September 30, 2026 it bills at the regional rate shown in brackets. See [the October 1, 2026 entry](/pricing-and-billing/whatsapp-pricing/rate-card#october-1-2026) for what changes.

## Codes that need care

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="+1 is shared across five markets">
    The United States and Canada each have their own rate card row, and they share the same rate. Puerto Rico, the Dominican Republic and Jamaica also use `+1` but bill at **Rest of Latin America**, which is more expensive for Marketing. Tell them apart by the three digits after the `+1`: `787`/`939` is Puerto Rico, `809`/`829`/`849` is the Dominican Republic, and `658`/`876` is Jamaica.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="+7 covers Russia and Kazakhstan">
    Both countries use `+7`. Kazakh mobile numbers start `+7 6` or `+7 7`; Russian mobile numbers start `+7 9`. Until September 30, 2026 Kazakhstan bills at the **Other** rate, because Meta's current mapping doesn't list it. From October 1, 2026 it gets its own row.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Longest match wins">
    Some codes are prefixes of others — `1` and `1 809`, `9` isn't a code but `91`, `92`, `94` and `971` all are. Always match the **longest** code that fits the start of the number, so `+971...` reads as the United Arab Emirates, not as a `+97` that doesn't exist.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  **Where this comes from.** This mapping mirrors [Meta's country calling code list](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing#country-calling-codes). If Meta adds a market or moves a country between regions, update this page alongside the rate card tables.
</Note>
