> **Who can use this?**
>
> - Billing management is available to all plans.
> - Only the **Account Owner** can change plans, update payment methods, view invoices, or cancel subscriptions.

<Info>
  Gallabox is billed on a subscription model — you pay for your plan quarterly or annually, and it unlocks the features in that plan. WhatsApp messaging and AI feature usage are billed separately, as usage-based add-ons — see [Pricing & Billing Overview](/pricing-and-billing/overview) for how the three fit together.
</Info>

## Plan Overview

| Plan | Best for | Key features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Basic** | Small teams getting started with WhatsApp automation | 3 users, 1 channel each (WhatsApp, Instagram and Web widget), basic bot nodes, Shopify integration, 500 AI credits/month |
| **Essential** | Growing businesses with advanced automation needs | 6 users, additional channels, API call node, CRM integrations, Google Sheets, GPT Dialog, GPT Knowledge Base, 2,000 AI credits/month |
| **Advanced** | Enterprises with AI-powered conversations | 10 users, everything in Essential plus AI voice, custom roles, number masking, 8,000 AI credits/month |

<Info>
  If you signed up earlier, you may be on a **legacy plan** (Starter, Team, Growth, Scale, or Pro). These are grandfathered — no longer sold, but they remain active for existing customers. Contact support to check your plan details or migrate to a current plan.
</Info>

## Billing Cycle

Gallabox bills you on a **quarterly** or **annual** cycle:

- **Quarterly billing:** Charged once every 3 months on the same date (e.g., June 5 → September 5)
- **Annual billing:** Charged once per year — typically includes a discount vs quarterly

**What's included in your plan fee:**

- Access to all features in your plan
- Your plan's included users (Basic 3, Essential 6, Advanced 10)
- Unlimited conversations processed
- Your plan's monthly AI credit allowance
- Support (email, chat — priority for Advanced)

**Billed separately — not part of your plan fee:**

- WhatsApp per-message charges (Marketing, Utility, Authentication), deducted from your prepaid [Message Credits](/pricing-and-billing/message-credits) wallet
- AI credit usage beyond your plan's monthly allowance, topped up via the [AI Wallet](/pricing-and-billing/ai-wallet)
- Additional users and channels, available as add-ons on Essential and Advanced plans

## Managing Your Subscription

### Viewing your current plan

Go to **Settings → Workspace → Subscription** — shows your current plan, billing cycle, next renewal date, and cost.

### Viewing invoices

Go to **Settings → Workspace → Subscription** and click **View Invoices** to see and download your invoices.

### Updating payment method

1. Go to **Settings → Workspace → Subscription**.
2. Click **Manage Payment Methods**.
3. Add a new card or update your existing one — it's used for all future charges.

**Accepted payment methods:** Credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard).

### Upgrading your plan

1. Go to **Settings → Workspace → Subscription**.
2. Choose your billing cycle (quarterly or annual).
3. Click **Upgrade** on the plan you want (Essential or Advanced).
4. Review the prorated charge for the remainder of the current billing period.
5. Complete the payment → plan is upgraded immediately.

**Worked example:** upgrading from Essential (₹X/month) to Advanced (₹Y/month) on day 10 of a 30-day cycle charges the difference for the remaining 20 days: (Y − X) × 20 ÷ 30.

Nothing is lost when you upgrade — existing flows, integrations, and data are preserved, and new features are available immediately.

### Downgrading your plan

1. Go to **Settings → Workspace → Subscription**.
2. Click **Downgrade** on the lower plan you want.
3. Confirm the downgrade.

If your account is using more than the lower plan allows — for example, more users or channels — you'll be asked to remove the extras before you can downgrade.

**Before downgrading, check:**

- Bot flows that use nodes from the higher plan (e.g., API Call) can't be published again until you remove those nodes
- Integrations unique to the higher plan will stop working
- Your monthly AI credit allowance will decrease

### Canceling your subscription

1. Go to **Settings → Workspace → Subscription** to open the **Plans & Subscription** page.
2. Scroll down and click **Cancel Subscription**.
3. Select your cancellation reason, then click **Proceed to cancellation**.

Your subscription is then **Scheduled for cancellation**. Your plan stays active until your current subscription period ends, and is canceled after that.

To keep your subscription, click **Remove Scheduled Cancellation** on the same page any time before your current subscription period ends.

You'll receive a confirmation email from our billing partner, **Chargebee** — this is a legitimate Gallabox email, even though the branding is Chargebee's. Invoice and payment-method screens are also Chargebee-hosted.

**What happens when you cancel:**

- You keep access until the end of your current billing period
- After that, your account moves to a read-only state (no new conversations, no bot flows)
- Data is retained for 30 days after cancellation (contact support to export before deletion)
- You can reactivate at any time during the retention period

## Team Billing

Each plan includes a set number of users — **Basic** 3, **Essential** 6, **Advanced** 10. On Essential and Advanced, you can add more users as add-ons from **Settings → Workspace → Subscription → Buy Addons**.

**Team roles:**

- **Owner** — full account control, including billing and subscription changes
- **Admin** — can manage settings and all features, except billing
- **Member** — can manage conversations and use assigned features
- **Custom roles** — available on selected plans; create them under **Settings → Workspace → Users, Teams & Roles → Roles**

## GST and TDS (India)

- **GST** is applied based on your billing address and itemized on each invoice. If a credit note or invoice isn't reflected in the GST portal, contact support with the invoice number.
- **TDS:** if your business deducts TDS on payments to Gallabox, contact support to share the TDS certificate and request acknowledgment or a refund claim.
- If you have a tax exemption certificate, contact support to update your billing profile.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch from quarterly to annual billing?">
    Yes — go to **Settings → Workspace → Subscription**, select **Annually** as the billing cycle, and choose your plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my bot flows when I downgrade?">
    If you have more bot flows than the lower plan allows, you'll need to delete the extra flows before you can downgrade.

    After you downgrade, your published flows keep working as they are. However, if a flow uses a node that isn't included in your new plan (for example, the API Call node when moving to Basic), you won't be able to publish any further changes to that flow until you remove that node or upgrade again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I was charged the wrong amount — what do I do?">
    Check your invoice first at **Settings → Workspace → Subscription → View Invoices**. If it still looks wrong, contact support with your invoice number.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
