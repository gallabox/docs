<Note>
  This page covers messages sent **through Gallabox** (the WhatsApp Cloud API). If you have [WhatsApp Coexistence](/whatsapp/whatsapp-coexistence) enabled, messages sent directly from the **WhatsApp Business App** on your phone are free and don't follow the rules below.
</Note>

## The 24-hour service window

Every WhatsApp conversation with a customer has a **24-hour window** that opens the moment they message you.

- **Customer-initiated** (the customer messages you first) — you can reply for free within 24 hours, using supported message types (text, images, and so on).
- **Business-initiated** (you message them first, or more than 24 hours have passed since their last message) — you can only reach them with a **template message**, which Meta must pre-approve. Every delivered template costs credits.

<Info>
  The 24-hour window resets each time the customer sends a new message. Replies remain free for as long as the conversation continues.
</Info>

| Message type | Category | Free inside the 24-hour window | Outside the window |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free-form message | — (not a template) | Free | Can't be sent |
| Template | Marketing | Charged | Charged |
| Template | Utility | Free | Charged |
| Template | Authentication | Free | Charged |

## Template categories

Every template belongs to one of three categories, and Meta requires you to pick one when you create it:

| Category | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Utility** | Following up on something the customer already did — an order, a booking, an account |
| **Authentication** | A one-time passcode or login verification code |
| **Marketing** | Promotions, offers, announcements, "come back and buy" messages |

<Warning>
  You choose the category when you create the template, but Meta checks that the content actually matches. A "utility" template that reads like a promotion can get moved to Marketing automatically — see [Template Categorization](/whatsapp/templates/template-categorization) for what qualifies as each category and what happens if Meta disagrees with your choice.
</Warning>

## What it costs, by country

Once a message is charged, the amount is determined by **the recipient's country**. Currency is based on your business's message credit currency.

<Warning>
  Example: an Indian business (billed in INR) sending a Marketing template to a customer with a UK number is charged the **UK rate**, not the India rate, read off the INR table below. Your country decides which currency column you read from; it never decides which row.
</Warning>

### Authentication pricing across borders

The Authentication rates above apply when the passcode is sent to a number in the same country as your business. The following countries charge a **higher** rate when the passcode is sent to a number in a different country: Egypt, India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Nigeria, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, and the UAE. Contact support for the exact cross-border rate if this applies to you.

## Ad Engagement Window

If a customer starts chatting with you from a WhatsApp ad or a Facebook/Instagram Page button, they get a longer free window of 72 hours compared to standard 24 hours — see [Ad Engagement Window](/guide/ad-engagement-window).

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why was I charged for a Utility or Authentication template I expected to be free?">
    These are only free when sent inside the customer's 24-hour window. Check the timestamp of the customer's last message — if more than 24 hours had passed, the send was business-initiated and charged.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I have WhatsApp Coexistence enabled — does this pricing apply to messages from my phone?">
    No. Pricing on this page only applies to messages sent through Gallabox. Messages sent directly from the WhatsApp Business App on your phone are always free — see [WhatsApp Coexistence](/whatsapp/whatsapp-coexistence).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My template's category changed after it was approved — will I now be charged differently?">
    Yes — Meta can recategorize an approved template if its content doesn't match the original category, most often Utility moving to Marketing. That changes what you're charged for it going forward. See [Template Categorization](/whatsapp/templates/template-categorization).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is my Authentication rate higher than what's shown in the table?">
    The table reflects same-country Authentication pricing. A small set of countries charge a higher rate when the passcode is sent across borders — see [Authentication pricing across borders](#authentication-pricing-across-borders) above.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
