<Warning>

This page describes Meta's **retired** pricing model and is kept for historical reference only.

</Warning>

Before July 2025, Meta charged WhatsApp Business API usage **per conversation** — a 24-hour messaging session opened by a customer message or a business-initiated template, billed once by category (Marketing, Utility, Authentication, or Service).

Meta has retired this model in favor of **per-message pricing**: each template message is charged individually based on its category, and free-form replies within the 24-hour customer service window remain free.

For current pricing, categories, rates, and cost-reduction tips, see [Message Credits](/pricing-and-billing/message-credits).
