> **Who can use this?**
>
> - Viewing your Message Credits balance is available to all plans.
> - Topping up, viewing detailed transaction history, and managing billing require a role with the relevant permissions, such as **Account Owners** and **Admins**.

<Info>
  Message Credits are a prepaid wallet that funds your WhatsApp messaging — similar to a prepaid phone plan. When it's empty, WhatsApp messages stop sending until you top up. For what actually gets charged and why, see [How WhatsApp Message Pricing Works](/pricing-and-billing/whatsapp-pricing/overview).
</Info>

## Usage history

Message Credits transaction history covers Free conversations, business-initiated messages, and customer-initiated messages, and can be exported for up to the last 6 months at any time — useful for auditing spend by category or campaign.

## Checking your balance

Go to **Settings → Billing → Message Credits** to see your current balance and full transaction history.

## Topping up

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Message Credits**.
2. Enter the amount you want to add and click **Add Credits**.
3. Complete the payment.

You'll get an in-app notification and an email confirmation once the top-up completes.

- Recharge any amount from **₹100 / 2,000** per top-up.
- Payment is by the card on file for your subscription, or via Razorpay.
- **18% GST** applies to INR top-ups.
- New accounts may start with free trial or onboarding credits already in the wallet.

## What gets deducted

Message Credits are deducted for every message Meta bills, including:

- **Templates** — Marketing, Utility and Authentication, from broadcasts, sequences, bots, the API and the inbox.
- **Service messages** — free-form replies inside the 24-hour window, from agents, bots, AI agents, canned responses, welcome and away messages, and reactions. Since October 1, 2026, these are charged after the first 1,000 service messages per channel each calendar month.
- **Utility templates sent inside the 24-hour window.**

A service message is charged at the Utility rate for your customer's country. Deductions are the same whether the message is sent from the web app, the mobile app or the conversation widget. For rates and what stays free, see [How WhatsApp Message Pricing Works](/pricing-and-billing/whatsapp-pricing/overview#your-1000-free-service-messages-a-month).

## What happens if your balance runs out

- Once your balance reaches zero or goes below it, you lose access to sending messages — including replying in conversations, AI replies, bot messages (including welcome and away messages) and running broadcasts — until you top up.
- In the inbox, the message box is blocked and shows **No message credits left. Add credit to send messages.** with an **Add Credit** link to the Message Credits page. Only users whose role has permission to top up can add credits; other users need to ask an Owner or Admin.
- A **scheduled broadcast** that doesn't have enough balance to complete will not send, and shows a failed status rather than partially sending.
- When you create a broadcast, Gallabox estimates its cost from the template's category and the number of contacts. If your balance doesn't cover it, a warning shows your balance and the approximate cost, with a **Recharge Now** button. After sending, open the broadcast to see its **Spend Estimate**.
- Each message is charged at the category Meta bills it under. If Meta bills a template at a higher rate than its category suggested — for example, after [recategorizing a Utility template to Marketing](/pricing-and-billing/whatsapp-pricing/overview#faqs) — the broadcast can cost more than the estimate, and your balance can go below zero.

## Low-balance notifications

Gallabox shows a banner in the app and sends an email when your balance falls below your **low balance alert** amount, so a broadcast or conversation doesn't fail mid-send. The alert is a warning only. Sending is blocked when your balance reaches zero or goes below it.

To change the alert amount:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Message Credits**.
2. On the balance card, click the edit icon next to **Low balance alert**.
3. Enter the amount and click **Save**.

If you haven't set an amount, Gallabox uses a default (₹100 or $5, depending on your wallet currency). Only users who can recharge the wallet can change it, and it applies to prepaid wallets only.

In the inbox, a **View credits** link below the message box shows your balance, so agents can see it before it runs out.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why wasn't my broadcast sent?">
    If your Message Credits balance was insufficient at send time, the broadcast is not sent and shows a **failed** status rather than sending partially. Top up and resend.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is my balance negative after a broadcast?">
    Charges are taken from your balance as Meta bills each message. If a broadcast costs more than your balance — most often because Meta billed its template as Marketing instead of Utility — the balance goes below zero. Sending stops until you top up, and your top-up first covers the negative amount. Top up more than the shortfall to start sending again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do unused Message Credits expire?">
    No — your balance carries forward; it's a prepaid wallet, not a monthly allowance that resets.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can anyone on my team top up Message Credits?">
    Only users whose role has the relevant permissions can add credits or view the detailed billing screen. Account Owners and Admins have them. Other roles can't access **Settings → Billing** unless an Admin grants the permissions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
