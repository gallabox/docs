<Info>
  Message Credits are a prepaid wallet that funds your WhatsApp messaging — similar to a prepaid phone plan. When it's empty, WhatsApp messages stop sending until you top up. WhatsApp charges **per message**, based on the message's category and the recipient's country, and whether it's sent inside or outside the customer's 24-hour service window — all explained below.
</Info>

## How WhatsApp message pricing works

### The 24-hour service window

When a customer messages your business, it opens a **24-hour service window**. The window resets every time the customer sends you a new message — it's a rolling window, not a fixed one.

Inside this window, you're talking to someone who just reached out to you. That's reflected in what's free:

| Message type | Inside the 24-hour window | Outside the window (business-initiated) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free-form text (normal replies) | **Free** | Can't be sent — a template is required to re-open contact |
| Utility template | **Free** | Charged |
| Authentication template | **Free** | Charged |
| Marketing template | Charged | Charged |

**The takeaway:** if you're replying to a customer who messaged you in the last 24 hours, Utility and Authentication templates cost nothing — same as a plain text reply. You only pay for them once you're the one re-opening the conversation after the window has closed. Marketing templates are billed every time, since they're inherently promotional rather than a reply to the customer.

<Note>
  This is Gallabox's current per-message pricing model. An older, retired model billed by 24-hour _conversation_ rather than by individual message — see [Conversation Pricing (Legacy)](/pricing-and-billing/conversation-pricing) if you're looking at historical invoices from before that change.
</Note>

### Template categories

- **Marketing** — promotional content, offers, product announcements, re-engagement campaigns. Always charged.
- **Utility** — transactional updates tied to an existing transaction: order confirmations, shipping updates, appointment reminders, account alerts. Free inside the service window, charged outside it.
- **Authentication** — one-time passcodes and identity verification codes. Free inside the service window, charged outside it.

### Ad-originated conversations

If a customer starts messaging you from a WhatsApp ad or a Facebook/Instagram Page button, a different — and longer — free window applies. See [Ad Engagement Window](/guide/ad-engagement-window) for how that works and how it differs from the standard 24-hour window above.

## Managing your Message Credits wallet

### Checking your balance

Go to **Settings → Billing → Message Credits** to see your current balance and full transaction history.

### Topping up

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Message Credits**.
2. Enter the amount you want to add and click **Add Credits**.
3. Complete the payment.

You'll get an in-app notification and an email confirmation once the top-up completes.

- Recharge any amount from **₹100 / $5** up to **₹1,00,000 / $2,000** per top-up.
- Payment is by the card on file for your subscription, or via Razorpay.
- **18% GST** applies to INR top-ups.
- New accounts may start with free trial or onboarding credits already in the wallet.

### What happens if your balance runs out

- If your balance is at zero or negative, you lose access to sending messages — including replying in conversations and running broadcasts — until you top up.
- A **scheduled broadcast** that doesn't have enough balance to complete will not send, and shows a failed status rather than partially sending.
- Gallabox estimates the credits a new broadcast will use before you send it, so you can check your balance covers it in advance.

### Low-balance notifications

Gallabox can notify your account's Owners and Admins — in-app and by email — before your balance runs out, so a broadcast or conversation doesn't fail mid-send. This isn't on by default for every account; if you'd like proactive low-balance alerts turned on, ask support to enable it.

### Usage history

Message Credits transaction history covers Free conversations, business-initiated messages, and customer-initiated messages, and can be exported for up to the last 6 months at any time — useful for auditing spend by category or campaign.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why am I charged for a message I thought was free?">
    Check whether it was sent inside or outside the customer's 24-hour service window, and which category it falls under — Marketing is always charged, while Utility and Authentication are only charged outside the window.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why wasn't my broadcast sent?">
    If your Message Credits balance was insufficient at send time, the broadcast is not sent and shows a **failed** status rather than sending partially. Top up and resend.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do unused Message Credits expire?">
    No — your balance carries forward; it's a prepaid wallet, not a monthly allowance that resets.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can anyone on my team top up Message Credits?">
    Only Account Owners and Admins can add credits or view the detailed billing screen. Other roles can't access **Settings → Billing**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
