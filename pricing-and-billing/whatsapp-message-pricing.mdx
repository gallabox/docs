<Info>
  This page covers messages sent **through Gallabox** . If you have WhatsApp Coexistence enabled, messages sent directly from the **WhatsApp Business App** on your phone are free and don't follow the rules below.
</Info>

WhatsApp pricing depends on the conversation window, type of message and category, and your customer's country.

## Template categories

Every template belongs to one of three categories, chosen when you create it:

| Category | What it's for |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Utility** | Following up on something the customer already did — an order, a booking, an account |
| **Authentication** | A one-time passcode or login verification code |
| **Marketing** | Promotions, offers, announcements, "come back and buy" messages |

You choose the category, but Meta checks that the content actually matches it — a "Utility" template that reads like a promotion can get recategorized to Marketing automatically, which changes what it costs going forward.

# The 24-hour service window

Every conversation has a rolling **24-hour window** that opens the moment a customer messages you, and resets each time they message you again.

While the window is open, you can reply with a **service message** — a free-form message like text, an image, a video, or a quick-reply button, sent without waiting for Meta's approval. Once the window closes, you can only reach that customer with a pre-approved **template message**.

**What's charged, by message type:**

| Message type | Category | Charge status | Free in the 24-hour service window |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Template | Marketing | Live | No |
| Template | Utility | Live | Free until October 1, 2026 |
| Template | Authentication | Live | No |
| Service message | — (not a template) | Live from October 1, 2026 | Free until October 1, 2026 |

<Check>
  Every business phone number also gets **1,000 free service messages a month** from October 1, 2026 — Meta only starts charging from the 1,001st. This resets each calendar month and doesn't carry over.
</Check>

## What it costs, by country

Once a message is charged, the rate depends on the **recipient's country**, read from the currency tab matching your account's billing currency.

<Info>
  Example: a business billed in INR sending a Marketing template to a UK number is charged the **UK row**, read from the **INR tab**. Your billing currency picks the tab; the recipient's country picks the row — never the other way around.
</Info>

Rates below are effective **October 1, 2026**.

Service messages bill at the same rate as Utility — use the Utility column for those too.

## Ad Engagement Window

If a customer starts chatting with you from a WhatsApp ad or a Facebook/Instagram Page button, you get a longer free window — **72 hours** instead of the usual 24.

<Check>
  Every message you send in that window is free, including templates, and that doesn't change on October 1, 2026.
</Check>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's changing on October 1, 2026?">
    Service messages and Utility templates sent inside the window start billing per message, at the market's Utility rate. Each business phone number also gets 1,000 free service messages a month before charges kick in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Am I billed for messages that failed to deliver?">
    Meta charges only for messages that have been successfully delivered, irrespective of conversation window, message type, etc.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I have WhatsApp Coexistence enabled — does this pricing apply to messages from my phone?">
    No. This page covers messages sent through Gallabox. Messages sent directly from the WhatsApp Business App on your phone are always free.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My template's category changed after approval — will I be charged differently now?">
    Yes. Meta can recategorize an approved template if its content doesn't match the category it was created under — most often, Utility moving to Marketing. That changes what it costs going forward.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do images or attachments cost more than plain text?">
    No. Media doesn't change a template's rate — you're charged per message, based on category, not content type.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
