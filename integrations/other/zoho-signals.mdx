title: "Zoho Signals"
description: "Get real-time notifications on your Zoho CRM for your chats with Zoho Signals. Stay informed even when your chat is closed. Don't miss a thing!"
---

Zoho Signals introduces a notification system that keeps users informed when their chat is not in an open state. Notifications are triggered 5 to 10 seconds after closing an open conversation. Notably, conversations lasting between 5 to 10 seconds do not prompt notifications.

<Info>

**Note:**

1. In Zoho, we send templates based on the chosen configuration. Whether you pick zoho.phone or zoho.mobile, it will work for bulk messages.
2. In the WhatsApp widget, if there is no configuration, the widget takes the value from the zoho.phone if it is available; otherwise, it takes from the zoho.mobile.
3. This feature is exclusive to the **Lead module** within Zoho CRM.

</Info>

### Integration Guide for Zoho Signals with Gallabox

1. From the left navigation go to **Integration** and scroll on **Zoho Signals** from the available apps. Click on **Connect**.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/zoho-signal-integration-app.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

2. Provide the Integration name and click on Submit. You will be redirected to sign in on your Zoho Account.
3. Sign in on Zoho and authorize for it to integrate with your Gallabox account.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/zoho-signal-authorization.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

4. Select the Channel or Channels (WhatsApp number) you would like to use with the **Zoho Signals** to send the messages to your customers and see if you have Enabled Signals as shown in the GIF.

<Check>

This enable users to select multiple channels for Zoho Signals integration.

</Check>

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/add-channels-zoho-signals.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

<Info>

If you don't want to receive the signals for any channel, remove that channel from the configuration.

</Info>

Once you update the configuration, you will start getting the signals when you receive a reply to the WhatsApp Message.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/update-configuration-zoho-signal.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

<Warning>

If you are not getting any notifications:

1. Make sure you have updated the Gallabox Application on the Zoho Marketplace.
2. Verify if the channel is integrated with Zoho Signals in Gallabox.

</Warning>
