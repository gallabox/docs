Below are the steps to follow in order to connect Zapier with Gallabox.

### Access Zapier from Gallabox

1. Click on the Integrations in the left navigation Pane of Gallabox. Choose Zapier from the Available apps and click on the Integration.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/zapier-with-gallabox.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

2. You will be taken to another window asking you to accept the invite from Gallabox if you have signed in on your Zapier Account in another browser.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/youve-been-invited-to-gallabox.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

3. In the other scenario, it will redirect you to the sign in page of Zapier like shown below. You have to sign in and then you will get the invitation to accept.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/zapier1-0.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

### Connect Gallabox in your Zapier Account

1. Once the invite is accepted, you will be redirected again to your Zapier account. From the left navigation, go to **Apps** and search Gallabox. Click on Connect.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/gallabox-app-in-zapier.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

2. A pop-up will open up asking you the [Account Id](https://app.gallabox.com/account) of Gallabox, the [API Key](https://github.com/mangoleaphq/gallabox-docs/tree/main/developer-resources/api-key-and-secret.md#create-an-api-key-in-gallabox), and the [API Secret](https://github.com/mangoleaphq/gallabox-docs/tree/main/developer-resources/api-key-and-secret.md#api-secret). You have to paste those and click on **Yes, Continue to Gallabox**.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/auth-zapier.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

Once connected, your Gallabox account will be available in Zapier, under My apps.
