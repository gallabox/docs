### Access Google Sheets from your Gallabox Account

1. From the left navigation go to **Integration** and Click on **Google Sheets** from the available apps. You will be redirected to **Make a Copy** of the Google Sheet.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/google-sheet-integration.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

2. Click on **Make a Copy** and rename the Google Sheet from _Copy of Gallabox WhatsApp Broadcast v221010.0_ to your desired name. Then, start adding the contact details for the broadcast.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/create-copy-of-the-google-sheet.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

3. Once you have added the contact details like Name and the phone number, click on the Gallabox icon as shown in the below screenshot. Then click on WhatsApp Broadcast.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/click-on-broadcast-icon.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

4. You will be prompted to authorize the Gmail account linked with your Gallabox account. Click **Continue**, then select **Advanced**. Click **Gallabox WhatsApp Unsafe** to proceed. Grant the necessary permissions to your Google account.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/sign-in-sheet-broadcast.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

### Configure the Gallabox Account in Google Sheet

1. Once you have permission, click the Gallabox icon again, then select WhatsApp Broadcast. Enter your Gallabox email and password, then click Submit.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/add-gallabox-credentials.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

2. To configure your account with your [API Secret](https://api-docs.gallabox.com/#api-secret) and [API Key](https://api-docs.gallabox.com/#create-an-api-key-in-gallabox), click on Account Config. You will be prompted to choose the number and add the corresponding details.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/add-api-key-and-secret.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

<Info>

If you have multiple phone numbers, they will be listed. Choose the number you want to use for the broadcast.

</Info>

3. After pasting the API Key and API Secret, return to Home in the pop-up and click **Run Once** or **Run on Edit** under Run Broadcast to share messages with the contacts listed in the Google Sheet.

<table><thead><tr><th width="165">Action</th><th>Description</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Run Once</td><td>Select this option to send the broadcast only once.</td></tr><tr><td>Run on Edit</td><td>Select this option if you are using Google Sheets for your broadcast campaigns. When a field, such as a WhatsApp number, is updated in the Google Sheet, the broadcast will be sent to that customer.</td></tr></tbody></table>

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/run-broadcast.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

By following these steps, you can efficiently automate your broadcast messages using Google Sheets.
