Pabbly provides you the ability to integrate multiple applications by managing the data flow smoothly. You can connect two or more apps with Gallabox and and send the Template Text Message via Gallabox. 

Let's see how you can connect Gallabox with 1000+ integrations using Pabbly Connect. 

### Access Pabbly from your Gallabox Account

1. From the left navigation go to **Integration** and Click on **Pabbly** from the available apps. 

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/pabbly-integration-in-gallabox.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

2. You will be redirected to the Pabbly Connect page which will ask you to connect your Gallabox account with 1000+ integrations. Click on Blue Button with Text **Connect Gallabox For Free** and it will redirect to the payment page if you don't have any Pabbly subscription else it will redirect you to your Pabbly account. 

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/connect-gallabox-with-pabbly.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

<Info>

If you don't have Pabbly account, take the subscription that will work for your business and then go to dashboard. 

</Info>

3. Click on the Create Workflow button to start creating workflows.

  <img src="/images/pabbly-1-4.png" alt="" />
</Frame>
