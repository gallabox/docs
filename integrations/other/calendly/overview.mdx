Want fewer missed appointments? Connecting your Calendly schedule to WhatsApp using Gallabox can help. It sends automatic reminders and confirmations, right to your clients' phones.

This way, they get a message where they're already looking – on WhatsApp. And you save time by not having to call or email reminders yourself.

**What will be achieved by this?**

* Clients get messages on WhatsApp, so they don't forget.
* They can "confirm" right in the WhatsApp Chat.
* More people show up for appointments.
* No more manual reminders.

### Before you Begin:

1. You will need a [Calendly](https://calendly.com/) account before proceeding with the Integration.
2. Add the Location (_Google meet, physical location, or any other link to join_) for the Event under Event details.

### Connect your Calendly Account with Gallabox

1. Go to **Integrations** from the Left Navigation. Scroll down to Available Integrations and Click on _Connect_ besides **Calendly**. 

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/calendly-integration-application.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

2. Once the **Calendly Integration Page** opens up, click on Connect again. Enter _Integration Name_ and click on Submit.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/connect-calendly.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

3. You will be directed to either Calendly's Sign in window (if you are not already signed in) or to the Integration Configuration Page (if you have signed in already on another tab).

> If you have not signed in to your Calendly account, sign in and then you will be redirected to the Calendly Configuration page in Gallabox. 

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/calendly-signin.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

> If you have signed in to your Calendly account, you will be redirected to the Calendly Configuration page in Gallabox.

4. If you want to change the Integration name, you can. Select the WhatsApp Channel you would like to use with "**Calendly Integration**". Click on **Update Configuration**. You will be taken to the Calendly Workflow page. You can create the _Workflows_ for **Reminder Messages**, **Confirmation Messages**, and share **follow-up messages** with your customers.
