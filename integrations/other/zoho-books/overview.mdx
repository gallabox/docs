<Info>

If you are on a **Premium plan** or above for Zoho Books, this integration will be possible. If you don't have any plan for Zoho Books, [click here](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/pricing/).

</Info>

### Connect Zoho Books with your Gallabox Account <a href="#connect-zoho-books-with-your-gallabox-account" id="connect-zoho-books-with-your-gallabox-account"></a>

1. Navigate to Integrations in Gallabox. Under Available Integrations, hover to Zoho Books and click on "**Connect**".

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/zoho-books-available-integration.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

2. Once the Integration Space opens up, click on "**Connect**" on the right side. You will be redirected to Zoho Books Sign-up page. Use the right credentials for your Zoho Books Account.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/sign-up-on-zoho-books.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

3. Provide the permissions to **READ,CREATE,UPDATE,DELETE** your Zoho Books data in Gallabox to proceed further. Click on "**Accept**". You will be redirected back to Gallabox.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/zoho-books-permission.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

4. Once on the Zoho Books Integration space on Gallabox, select the **Channel** you would like to connect with Zoho Books and Click on "**Update Configuration**". You will get a Authorization Token to be pasted in "[**Zoho Books Extension**](https://books.zoho.in/extension#/installprivateextension?name_space=61216625-db5e-4301-97f0-2151e4f36c9b\&handle=isx3m\&install_type=private\&version=4.1)".
5. Click on the link for Zoho Books Extension and you will be directed to Installation page for **Zoho Books**. Select your _Organization_ from the dropdown, agree to the Terms & Conditions, and click on "**Next**". You will be taken to a page where you have to paste the **authorization token** from Gallabox Integration Space.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/zoho-installation.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

6. Copy the Authorization token from Gallabox and paste it on the Zoho Books Installation Page. Click on "**Install Extension**".

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/install-extension-zoho-books.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

7. Go back to Gallabox's Integration Space and click on the button provided. Once you do, you will get your organization details (**Email, Name, and Organization ID**) confirming successful Integration.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/integration-successfull.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

Now you can go to Workflows and start configuring the WhatsApp Templates for the Zoho Books components.

<Warning>

Every Gallabox account can only be integrated with one Zoho Books account.

</Warning>
