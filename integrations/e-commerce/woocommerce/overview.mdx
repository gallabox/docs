### Overview

The WooCommerce Integration guide simplifies the process—connect effortlessly, provide essential details, and enjoy the benefits of a well-connected Gallabox and WooCommerce system for efficient business operations.

### WooCommerce Integration

1. Go to **Integrations** in the left navigation. Hover on **WooCommerce** Integration from the available applications and click on Connect.

<Frame caption="Integrations in Gallabox">
  <video src="/images/integration-woocommerce.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

2. Click on **Connect** and paste your shop URL click on **Submit.** It will ask you to Approve Gallabox.

_Make sure you are logged in on WooCommerce on another browser of your browser window._ 

<Frame caption="Connecting WooCommerce with Gallabox">
  <video src="/images/wc1.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

3. Provide the **Brand Name** and the shop URL. Select the **WhatsApp Channel** and then click on **Configure.** 

<Frame caption="Configuring the WooCommerce shop in Gallabox">
  <video src="/images/woocommerce-config-gallabox.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

_**Congratulations! You have successfully connected your WooCommerce with Gallabox.**_

### Delete Integration

If you want to delete the WooCommerce Integration due to some reasons, follow the 2 step process listed below.

1. Go to **Integration** on your Gallabox dashboard. Click on the **Integrated WooCommerce Application.**

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/woocommerce-integration.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

2. Click on the 3 dots besides Connected and you will find a delete option. When you click on Delete, the integration will be deleted and cannot be recovered.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/delete-woocommerce-integration.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

<Info>

**Heads up:**\
If you delete the integration, you won’t be able to reconnect it directly. You will need to set it up again from scratch.

</Info>
