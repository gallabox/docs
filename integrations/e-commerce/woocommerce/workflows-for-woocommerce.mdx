### Order Pending Status

To send the Order Pending notifications automatically, you will have to create a workflow under the WooCommerce Integration with Gallabox. Let's delve into that.

1. Go to **Integration** on your Gallabox dashboard. Click on the **Integrated WooCommerce Application.**

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/woocommerce-in-gallabox.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

2. Go to Workflow. Click on Use button beside **Order Pending Workflow**, and choose a **Message Template** that you want to send to your customers when customers add the product in their cart.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/wc3.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

3. Map the **variables** for the selected message templates and click on **Submit.**

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/wc31.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

4. Add the relevant **Send After** time.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/wc32.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>
