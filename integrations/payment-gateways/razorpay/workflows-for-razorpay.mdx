### Payment Capture Alerts

To send the payment notifications automatically when a payment is captured, you will have to create a workflow under the Razorpay Integration with Gallabox. Let's delve into that.

1. Go to **Integration** on your Gallabox dashboard and from My Integration Section , choose the connection under the Razorpay Integration.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/connected-razorpay-integration.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

2. Click on Use button beside **Payment Captured Workflow**, and choose a **Message Template** that you want to send to your customers when they make a payment to your business. Map the right **variables** for the selected message template and add the payload for the button if you would like to trigger a bot on button reply.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/send-whatsapp-template-payment-captured-razorpay.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

3. Add the Action like updating the contact tag with the Tag like `payment made` to update the contact detail and click on **Submit.**

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/action-for-payment-captured-razorpay.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

_**Great! Now you are good to go with the template.** Whenever you receive a payment, a message will be shared with the customers._

<Frame>

</Frame>

### Remove Razorpay Integration

If you want to delete the Razorpay Integration due to some reasons, follow the 2 step process listed below.

1. Go to **Integration** on your Gallabox dashboard. Click on the **Integrated Razorpay Application.**
2. Click on the 3 dots besides Connected and you will find a delete option. When you click on Delete, the integration will be deleted and cannot be recovered.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/rp10.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

_**Congratulations! You have successfully deleted the integration.**_

### Disable the Workflows

If you want to disable the Razorpay workflow due to some reasons, just switch off the toggle beside the workflow.
