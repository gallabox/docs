### Order Shipped Notifications

To send the order shipped notifications automatically, you will have to create a workflow under the ShipRocket Integration with Gallabox. Let's delve into that.

1. Go to **My Integration**. Click on the **ShipRocket Integration** and choose the connection name that you would like to use.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/shiprocket-integration.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

2. Click on the toggle button beside **Order Shipped Workflow.**

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/shiprocket1-2.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

and choose a **Message Template** that you want to send to your customers when they make a payment to your business.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/shiprocket-1-8.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

3. Map the **variables** for the selected message templates and click on **Submit.**

_**Great! Now you are good to go with the template.** Whenever the order is shipped, a message will be shared with your customers._
