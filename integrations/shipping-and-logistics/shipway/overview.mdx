### **Connect Shipway One with Shipway Experience**

1. Login in to Shipway One Dashboard ([https://shipway.com/](https://shipway.com/)) and click on Setting on the top right side of the dashboard. 

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/shipway-dashboard.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

2. Under Integrations, click on 3rd party Integrations. Click on **+Add New third Party.** 

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/shipway-third-party-integrations.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

3. From the options, click on Shipway Experience and paste **UserName** and **Licence Key** from your Shipway Experience dashboard. Click on Save Settings and Shipway Experience will be connected with your Shipway One Account. 

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/shipway-third-party-integration.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

### **Connect the Integration**

1. From the left navigation go to **Integration** and Click on **Shipway** from the available apps. 

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/shipway-integration-app.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

2. After clicking on the Shipway Integration app, click on **Connect**. 

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/shipway-integration-connect.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

3. Enter the Integration Name, Username, and Password to connect the Integration. The username has to be taken from your Shipway account. In place of Password, you have to enter the **License Key** available on the shipway account. Go to [https://dashboard.shipway.in/BS](https://dashboard.shipway.in/BS) to get the Username and License Key. 

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/shipway-settings.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

4. Once you have entered the username and License Key in place of username and Password in Gallabox, click on Connect and you will land on the configuration page. 

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/shipway-integration-connected.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

### **Configuring the Integration**

1. Select the Channel (WhatsApp number) you would like to use with the Shipway Integration to send the messages to your customers. 

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/shipway-integration-confi.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

2. After selecting the channel, click on update configuration. 

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/shipway-integration-updated.png" alt="" />
</Frame>
