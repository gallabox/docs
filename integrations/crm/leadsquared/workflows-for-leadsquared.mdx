### LeadSquared New Lead Automation

1. From the top navigation click on **Workflow** and select **Automation.** Click on **+ Create Automation** and then click on **Build from Scratch**.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/ls5.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

2. Click on **New Lead** and **Save**.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/ls6.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

3. Click on + and **Actions** drop-down will open and select **Messaging** and under that Click on the option **WhatsApp.** Select the desired template and click on **Save**.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/ls7.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

4. Click on Publish to send the selected message automatically be shared with the new lead that gets created.

<Info>

Make sure that the template message you want to share is already copy & pasted on your Leadsquared account from your Gallabox Account.

</Info>

### Broadcast Messages on LeadSquared

To send broadcast messages on LeadSquared:

1. Select the contacts that you would like to send WhatsApp Messages.
2. Click on Actions and choose the option - WhatsApp.
3. A pop-up will open up for you to choose the Channel, Name the Campaign, and Choose the WhatsApp Template to be shared.
4. Once you click on Send, you will get a confirmation that the messages were sent.
