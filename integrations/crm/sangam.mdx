<Info>

Before integrating Sangam CRM, make sure you are not on a free plan. 

</Info>

To connect your Leadsquared account with Gallabox follow the below steps:

1. From the left navigation go to **Integration** and locate the **Sangam CRM** from the available apps.

<Frame>
  <video src="/images/sangam-crm.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
</Frame>

2. When you click on the Sangam CRM application in Gallabox, you will be redirected to the Sangam CRM application. You can then login to your account and start automating with Gallabox.

  <video src="/images/sangam-login.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls className="w-full aspect-video" />
