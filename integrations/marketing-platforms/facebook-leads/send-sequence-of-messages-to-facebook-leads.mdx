---
title: "Send Sequence of Messages to Facebook Leads"
description: "Take control of your Facebook Leads with Gallabox. Learn how to use Action in a Workflow to trigger a sequencing of messages. Follow our step-by-step guide and optimize your lead generation process."
---

Let's see how you can use Action in a Workflow to trigger a sequencing of the messages for your Facebook Leads.

1. Go to **Workflow** under the Fb Integration. You click on **Create New Workflow** and select the desired **FB page.** Click on the **Lead Ad Form** that you want to use and then **Pick a Template.**

<Frame>
  <img src="https://2022268617-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FZcl3T6ePz95GpzjAeOLW%2Fuploads%2FKKrDNeGcWP1bDX2Yi6Fi%2Ffb5.gif?alt=media&token=34e4d413-8db0-44b2-a5fe-624372c8b3ef" alt=""  />
</Frame>

2. After selecting the **Template** put appropriate variables like Recipient's Name and phone number.Make sure to map the variables in a right way. Know More about variables here.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://2022268617-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FZcl3T6ePz95GpzjAeOLW%2Fuploads%2FHdVsw82Qpko90eOxte0M%2Ffb6.gif?alt=media&token=c727ccc6-993f-468a-8e18-74c27da83912" alt=""  />
</Frame>

3. Go to **Action** and select the **Action Type** as **Tags** (Choose Update Contact tags) and choose the tag you want to update. Select the **Operation** you want to perform and click on **Create Workflow.**

<Frame>
  <img src="https://2022268617-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FZcl3T6ePz95GpzjAeOLW%2Fuploads%2FStcW4jbSsYr3r2dpPC4m%2Ffb7.gif?alt=media&token=24d630a4-d5ab-4445-b7c2-553b6dee7e3d" alt=""  />
</Frame>

4. Go to Sequence in left navigation and search for the relevant sequence you want the customers to follow.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://2022268617-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FZcl3T6ePz95GpzjAeOLW%2Fuploads%2FdtRtD55Tp36CdF2V9Fq1%2Ffbseq.gif?alt=media&token=7d9e7888-ce2d-4cfd-a119-5ddf957d8006" alt=""  />
</Frame>

5. Select the Trigger as **On create or On update.** Set the conditions as **Tags**, select **Contains** and Pick the tag you have selected in step 03. Then click on Publish & Close.

<Info>

Know more about sequence feature in Gallabox [here](https://github.com/mangoleaphq/gallabox-docs/tree/main/whatsapp-drip-campaigns/basics-of-drip-campaign.md).

</Info>
