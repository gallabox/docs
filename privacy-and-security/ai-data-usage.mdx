> **Who can use this?**
>
> * This information applies to all Gallabox plans and roles.

<Info>

Gallabox's AI features send the data they need to third-party AI providers to generate a reply. AI only processes data in features you turn on or use. Your data is processed in the United States, and it isn't used to train AI models.

</Info>

## How it works

When you use an AI feature, Gallabox sends the AI provider only what it needs to write a reply. The provider sends the reply back to Gallabox.

To generate a reply, Gallabox sends the provider:

* **The current message** and **the conversation history**, so the reply fits the conversation
* **Contact details**, such as name and phone number, only when your AI agent's configuration uses them
* **Your instructions**, such as the agent's Role, Goal, and Instructions, or the prompt in a bot node
* **Relevant extracts from your Knowledge Base**, when the agent or node is connected to one
* **Call audio**, for calls answered by the Voice AI Agent

Your full [Knowledge Base](/ai-agents-and-bots/chat-ai-agent/knowledge-base/overview) never goes to the provider. Gallabox stores your files, website pages, and the search index built from them on its own infrastructure in AWS. Only the extracts that match the question are sent.

## AI providers

Gallabox uses AI models from:

* **OpenAI**
* **Google Cloud** (Gemini models), which also powers the Voice AI Agent
* **xAI**

Most requests go directly to the provider. Some features route requests through **Vercel AI Gateway** or **Amazon Bedrock** to one of these providers. All of these companies are listed as sub-processors in [Annex III of our DPA](/privacy-and-security/dpa#annex-iii).

## Features that use AI

* [Chat AI Agent](/ai-agents-and-bots/chat-ai-agent/overview) on WhatsApp, Instagram, and Web Chat
* The **GPT Dialog** and **GPT Knowledge Base** nodes in [bot flows](/ai-agents-and-bots/bots/nodes/flow-control-nodes)
* [AI Reply](/conversations/ai-reply), **AI Rewrite**, and **AI Translate** in the Inbox
* **Rewrite with AI** when you [create a template](/whatsapp/templates/custom-marketing)
* [Instagram Comment Automation](/instagram/comment-automation)
* The **Voice AI Agent** for [incoming WhatsApp calls](/whatsapp/calling/incoming-calls)

There's no account-wide switch to turn AI off. To stop AI processing, turn off or unassign your Chat AI Agents, remove GPT nodes from your bot flows, and stop using the AI tools in the Inbox.

## Retention and training

**AI providers** may keep requests and replies for **up to 30 days** to monitor for abuse, and then delete them. They don't use data sent through the API to train their models.

**Gallabox** keeps AI conversations, chat logs, and Knowledge Base content on the same retention schedule as the rest of your account data. See the [Privacy Policy](https://gallabox.com/privacy-policy) for details. Gallabox doesn't use your data to train AI models. It does use AI conversation data to review reply quality, improve AI features, produce usage analytics, and bill AI credits.
