> **Who can use this?**
>
> * Anyone setting up how a WhatsApp channel behaves when customers message you.
> * Roles: Owners and Admins, and custom roles with permission to manage channels.
> * Plans: Most configurations require the Essential or Advanced plan. Settings you do not have access to appear locked.

<Info>

Channel configuration is where you decide what happens automatically on a WhatsApp number — when you are open, what customers hear first, what happens when nobody replies, and when a chat closes itself. Each setting is a card you switch on and configure independently.

</Info>

## Before you start

* Connect the WhatsApp channel first — see [Connect your WhatsApp](/whatsapp/connect-your-whatsapp).
* Create the [canned responses](/whatsapp/canned-responses) you want to send automatically. Welcome, away and auto reply messages, and auto follow-ups in **Canned response** mode, all pick from canned responses rather than free text.
* Settings apply to **one channel**. If you run several WhatsApp numbers, configure each one.

## Where to find it

Go to **WhatsApp Channel → \[your channel]** in the left navigation, then open the **Configuration** tab.

The bot or AI agent that answers new conversations isn't set here. It's set in the channel's assignment rules. See [Set the default bot for new conversations](/ai-agents-and-bots/how-conversations-reach-a-bot#set-the-default-bot-for-new-conversations).

<Warning>

**If your channel is in coexistence mode** — running the WhatsApp Business App alongside Gallabox — turn the equivalent setting **off in the WhatsApp app first**. Business hours, welcome messages and away messages exist in both places, and leaving both on sends the customer two messages. See [WhatsApp coexistence](/whatsapp/whatsapp-coexistence).

</Warning>

## The settings

### Business hours

Tells customers when you are available, and drives when away messages are sent.

You can set:

* **Working hours** for each day of the week, or **Open 24/7**.
* **Special days** — different hours for a specific date or a date range, such as a late opening or a half day.
* **Closed days** — dates you are shut, such as public holidays.

### Away message

Sends an automatic reply when a customer messages outside your business hours, so nobody is left without an answer overnight.

Pick the canned response to send. Away messages depend on **Business hours** being configured — without hours, Gallabox does not know when you are away.

### Welcome message

Greets a customer with an automatic message the first time they reach you.

Pick the canned response to send. You can preview it before saving, and create a new canned response without leaving the page.

### Auto reply

Sends a holding message when a customer has been waiting without a reply — "thanks, someone will be with you shortly."

You configure:

* **Canned response** — the message to send.
* **Minutes** — how long to wait before sending it.
* **Preference** — which conversations it applies to: **Unassigned conversations**, **Assigned conversations**, or **For all conversations**.

<Tip>

Set the wait time slightly above your realistic first-response time. Too short and every customer gets a robotic message before a human would have replied anyway.

</Tip>

### Agent fallback management

Handles conversations that stall because an agent is slow or has gone offline. Two independent rules:

* **Handover Fallback** — runs when a chat is handed from one agent, bot or team to a team member, and no reply is sent in time. Under **When should this run?**, choose **Always** or **Only when the customer is waiting**.
* **Inactive Agent Fallback** — runs when a customer messages a team member who is marked **Away**, and no reply is sent in time.

For each rule you set:

* **Escalate after (minutes)** — how long to wait, from 1 to 1440.
* **Reassign the chat** — **Assign to Team**, **Assign to User**, **Assign to same team**, or **Assign to Chat Agent**.
* **Send a canned response** — an optional message to the customer when the rule runs.
* **Send a webhook** — notify an external system when the rule runs.

Both rules only act on conversations assigned to a person, so they don't fire while a bot is handling the chat. A reply from a team member or bot resets the countdown.

### Auto follow-up

Follows up automatically when an agent has messaged a customer and the customer stops replying. This setting was previously called **Re-engagement message**.

Under **How should we follow up?**, pick one mode:

* **Canned response** — sends one message you pick from your canned responses.
* **AI follow-up** — AI writes up to 3 short follow-ups from the chat, such as reminding the customer of the agent's question. Uses [AI credits](/pricing-and-billing/ai-wallet).

Both modes have **Send first follow-up after** — how many minutes (1–1439) to wait with no reply to the agent's last message.

For **Canned response**, pick the **Canned Response** to send. You can preview it before saving.

For **AI follow-up**, you also set:

* **Number of follow-ups** — 1, 2 or 3. The default is 2.
* **Wait between follow-ups** — how many minutes (1–1439) to wait after one follow-up before sending the next. The default is 4.
* **Instructions for AI** — optional guidance on tone, length or topics to avoid, up to 300 characters. For example: "Keep it friendly. Don't quote prices."

If **AI follow-up** isn't available on your account, you only see the canned response settings.

<Note>

Auto follow-up works only on open chats assigned to a team member. Follow-ups go out only inside WhatsApp's 24-hour customer service window. They are never sent as templates.

</Note>

#### How AI follow-ups work

* **When they start.** The wait starts when a team member sends a message from the Inbox in an open chat assigned to a team member. Each new message from your team starts the wait again from zero.
* **What the AI reads.** The AI reads the most recent messages in the chat and refers to what the agent asked. It doesn't use a knowledge base. It sees the customer's first name, but not their phone number or email.
* **When it doesn't follow up.** If the chat has clearly ended, such as with a goodbye or a confirmed order, the AI doesn't send a follow-up.
* **When they stop.** Follow-ups stop when the customer replies, or the chat is resolved, unassigned or handed to a bot or AI agent. They also stop when the 24-hour window closes, the contact opts out or is blocked, your AI credits run out, or you turn Auto follow-up off.
* **Messages sent outside the Inbox.** A message sent through the API or an integration stops any pending follow-ups but doesn't start new ones.
* **What agents see.** Each AI follow-up appears in the chat as a message from your business. A log line in the chat records when follow-ups start, are sent and stop, and why they stopped.
* **Assignment.** Follow-ups never change who the chat is assigned to. The customer's reply reaches the same team member.

Changes to these settings apply from the next message your team sends. Follow-ups already scheduled keep the settings they started with.

### Auto resolve chat

Resolves a conversation automatically when a team member replied and the customer hasn't answered within a set number of **Minutes** (1–1439). Your open queue then reflects work that's genuinely active.

* The countdown starts from a team member's message. On [Coexistence](/whatsapp/coexistence/overview) numbers, a message sent from the WhatsApp Business app also counts.
* It only resolves conversations assigned to a person. Messages sent by a bot don't start the countdown, so it never closes a chat a bot is handling. For bots, see [Bot wait time and drop-off](/ai-agents-and-bots/bots/wait-time-and-drop-off).
* Messages sent through the API, an integration, or a workflow don't start the countdown either. Only replies sent from the Inbox, the WhatsApp Business app, or an auto follow-up do.
* If **Auto follow-up** is on in **Canned response** mode and set to the same number of minutes, a team member's reply doesn't start the countdown. It starts when the follow-up is sent.
* If **Auto follow-up** is on in **AI follow-up** mode, auto resolve never closes a chat while follow-ups are still pending. The countdown starts again after the last follow-up is sent or the follow-ups stop on their own.

### Failed message email alert

Sends an email to an address you choose whenever a message fails on this channel. Useful for catching billing problems, quality restrictions and template pauses early rather than discovering them in a report.

See [Message error codes](/whatsapp/message-error-codes) for what the failures mean.

### Marketing opt-out messages

Handles customers who want to stop receiving marketing messages, and lets them subscribe back.

You configure:

* **Primary keyword** — the word that triggers opt-out.
* **Additional keywords** — other words that should also trigger it, such as `Unsubscribe`.
* **Opt-out confirmation message** — what the customer receives when they opt out.
* **Button text to subscribe back** — the label on the re-subscribe button.
* **Re-subscription confirmation** — what the customer receives when they opt back in.

### Image visibility

Controls how incoming images appear in conversations. Turn on **Hide all images** to hide images by default; agents can then unhide individual images as needed.

Useful where agents handle sensitive or unsolicited media, or where screens are visible to others.

### AI reply

Drafts suggested replies for agents using AI, based on knowledge sources you choose.

You configure:

* **Reply generation instruction** — how the AI should write, such as tone and length.
* **Knowledge base** — the sources the AI refers to when drafting replies in this channel.

AI reply requires the Advanced plan capability. Drafts are suggestions — an agent still reviews and sends them.

### Business app message settings

Appears only on channels in [coexistence mode](/whatsapp/whatsapp-coexistence). **Business App Managed By** chooses the user or team a conversation moves to when you reply from the WhatsApp Business app. This also takes the conversation from a bot. See [Bots and AI agents when you reply from the app](/whatsapp/coexistence/what-changes#bots-and-ai-agents-when-you-reply-from-the-app).

### Conversation initiation template

Available on some accounts. Sets the template used when an agent starts a brand-new conversation with a customer, along with the **button CTA** text.

## Suggested setup order

<Steps>

<Step title="Set business hours">
Everything time-based depends on this. Do it first.
</Step>

<Step title="Add welcome and away messages">
These cover the two moments a customer is most likely to be left waiting — their first message, and anything sent after hours.
</Step>

<Step title="Add auto reply and agent fallback">
Now handle the in-between case: a customer who reached you during working hours but is not getting a reply.
</Step>

<Step title="Turn on failed message alerts">
So you hear about delivery problems by email rather than finding them later.
</Step>

<Step title="Set marketing opt-out handling">
Required if you send marketing broadcasts, and it protects your number's quality rating.
</Step>

<Step title="Add auto resolve last">
Once the rest is working, close stale conversations automatically to keep the queue honest.
</Step>

</Steps>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Do these settings apply to all my WhatsApp numbers?">
No. Configuration is per channel. Set it up separately for each WhatsApp number.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why is a setting greyed out or locked?">
That setting is not included in your plan. Contact your account manager to find out what is available on a higher plan.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My customers are getting two welcome messages. Why?">
Your channel is almost certainly in coexistence mode with the setting turned on in both places. Turn the greeting message off in the WhatsApp Business App and keep it only in Gallabox.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between auto reply and auto follow-up?">
**Auto reply** covers a customer waiting on you — they messaged and nobody has replied yet. **Auto follow-up** covers the opposite — you replied and the customer went quiet.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where did the re-engagement message setting go?">
It's now called **Auto follow-up**, in the same place. If you used re-engagement message before, your settings carry over as **Canned response** mode with the same message, minutes and on or off state.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why didn't an AI follow-up go out?">
Open the chat and look for the auto follow-up log line. It shows why follow-ups stopped, such as the customer replied, the 24-hour window closed, the conversation had ended, or your AI credits ran out.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do automatic messages cost anything?">
Messages sent inside the 24-hour customer service window follow normal WhatsApp conversation pricing. See [WhatsApp pricing](/pricing-and-billing/overview).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do I configure WhatsApp calling?">
Calling is on its own **Calls** tab on the channel, not in Configuration. See [WhatsApp Calling](/whatsapp/calling/overview).
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
