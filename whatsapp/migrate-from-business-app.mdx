> **Who can use this?**
>
> * Gallabox users moving a WhatsApp Business App number to the WhatsApp Cloud API.
> * Roles: Owners and Admins — WhatsApp connection is configured in the Channels section.

<Info>

Migrating from the WhatsApp Business App to Gallabox moves your number fully onto the **WhatsApp Cloud API**. You keep the same phone number, and your team manages every conversation from Gallabox. The WhatsApp Business App **stops working** for that number.

</Info>

<Warning>

If you want to keep using the WhatsApp Business App on your phone **and** Gallabox on the same number, don't migrate. Use [WhatsApp Coexistence](/whatsapp/coexistence/overview) instead. Not sure which to choose? See [Coexistence or Cloud API?](/guide/coexistence-or-cloud-api)

</Warning>

## What changes when you migrate

|                  | WhatsApp Business App | Gallabox                                     |
| ---------------- | --------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Users**        | 1 person, on 1 phone  | Unlimited team members, shared inbox         |
| **Inbox**        | Personal phone        | Web-based team inbox                         |
| **Automation**   | Limited auto-replies  | Full chatbot flows, Chat AI Agent, sequences |
| **Broadcasts**   | Broadcast lists       | Bulk campaigns with targeting                |
| **Integrations** | None                  | CRM, API, webhook integrations               |
| **Analytics**    | None                  | Full reporting and session tracking          |

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Step 1: Back up your WhatsApp chat history">
Gallabox doesn't import chat history when you migrate, and deleting your WhatsApp account in the next step removes your chats from the app. Export any chats you want to keep first:

1. Open the **WhatsApp Business App** on your phone.
2. Go to **Settings → Chats → Chat History**.
3. Tap **Export Chat** for any chats you want to save.
4. Choose a destination, such as email or cloud storage.
</Step>

<Step title="Step 2: Delete your WhatsApp account from the app">
Meta doesn't let a number that's in use on WhatsApp be connected to the Cloud API. Delete the account from the app first:

1. In the **WhatsApp Business App**, go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**.
2. Enter your phone number and confirm.

<Warning>

Deleting your account can't be undone. Once it's deleted, the number can't be used with [Coexistence](/whatsapp/coexistence/overview), which needs the number to be active on the WhatsApp Business App. Decide between Coexistence and migrating before you do this.

</Warning>
</Step>

<Step title="Step 3: Connect the number to Gallabox">
Connect the number with the standard Cloud API setup. Follow [Connect Your WhatsApp](/whatsapp/connect-your-whatsapp) from **How to Connect**. You'll verify the number with a one-time code sent by SMS or voice call, so keep the SIM active and to hand.
</Step>

<Step title="Step 4: Set up your team">
Once the number is connected:

1. Go to **Settings → Team Members** in Gallabox.
2. Invite your team by email.
3. Assign roles (Admin, Agent) based on responsibilities.
4. Set up **assignment rules** so conversations route to the right people automatically.
5. Configure your **Chat AI Agent** or bot flows if you have automation set up.
</Step>

</Steps>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Will I lose my existing WhatsApp chat history?">
Gallabox doesn't import chat history when you migrate. New conversations after you connect appear in Gallabox. Export any chats you want to keep before you delete your account from the app. See [Step 1](#steps).

If you want your recent chats brought into Gallabox, use [Coexistence](/whatsapp/coexistence/overview), which can import up to 180 days of 1:1 chats.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I migrate without losing my phone number?">
Yes. Your phone number stays the same. Only where you manage it changes, from the WhatsApp Business App to Gallabox.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I keep using the WhatsApp Business App after migrating?">
No. After migrating, the number works only through Gallabox. To use the app and Gallabox on the same number, use [Coexistence](/whatsapp/coexistence/overview) instead.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if the number won't connect?">
Common reasons:

* **The number is still active on WhatsApp.** Delete the account from the app first. See [Step 2](#steps).
* **The number is connected to another WhatsApp Business API provider.** Disconnect it from that provider first.

See [Connect Your WhatsApp](/whatsapp/connect-your-whatsapp) for more troubleshooting, or contact Gallabox support.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
