> **Who can use this?**
>
> - Gallabox users with **channel management permissions**.
> - Roles: Owners and Admins
> - Plans: Available on all Gallabox plans.

<Warning>
  Coexistence is **not enabled by default**. Contact Gallabox support before starting — the feature must be activated on your workspace first. Do not start the standard connection flow if you want Coexistence.
</Warning>

## Before You Start

Make sure you have:

1. **WhatsApp Business App** version **2.24.17 or higher** installed on your phone
2. A phone number that is **active on the WhatsApp Business App** — new or barely used numbers can fail Meta's eligibility check
3. The number **not already connected to another WhatsApp API provider**. If it is, disconnect that provider first and wait **15 minutes** before retrying
4. **Gallabox Coexistence enabled** for your workspace (contact support)

<Info>
  Coexistence is available for most countries, including India, the United Kingdom, the EEA, Australia, Japan, the Philippines, South Korea, Turkey, Nigeria, and South Africa. A small number of regions remain unsupported — if your number is rejected with a region error, contact [Gallabox support](mailto:support@gallabox.com).
</Info>

## Back Up Your Chats First

Before you connect, back up your chat history on the phone. Gallabox imports a lot, but not everything, and the import cannot be re-run:

- Only the **180 days** before onboarding are imported
- Media older than **14 days** arrives as a placeholder, without the file
- Group chats are never imported
- If you decline history sharing, nothing is imported
- The import runs **once**. If it fails, you have to disconnect and start over

A backup on the phone covers all of these. To make one, open the **WhatsApp Business App** and go to **Settings → Chats → Chat backup → Back Up Now**. WhatsApp has full instructions for [Android](https://faq.whatsapp.com/744445782709185/) and [iPhone](https://faq.whatsapp.com/180225246548988/).

<Info>
  The backup stays on your phone and in your own Google Drive or iCloud account. Gallabox never reads it — it is your copy, kept in case you want something the import did not bring across.
</Info>

## Steps

Coexistence is set up in four stages. Make sure support has enabled Coexistence for your workspace before starting.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact Gallabox support">
    Email [support@gallabox.com](mailto:support@gallabox.com) with:

    - Your WhatsApp Business App phone number
    - A note that you want to enable Coexistence

    Gallabox will verify your eligibility and activate the feature on your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Eligibility check">
    The Gallabox team will confirm:

    - Your number is on the WhatsApp Business App (not already on Cloud API)
    - Your country is supported
    - No existing credit-line or partner conflicts on your number
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect through Gallabox">
    Once eligible, complete the onboarding through Gallabox:

    1. Start the connection flow in Gallabox and enter your WhatsApp Business App number. Meta recognises that the number is already on the app and switches to the Coexistence flow automatically. On some accounts you may instead be asked to choose the option to connect your existing WhatsApp Business App account — pick that.
    2. A **verification code** will appear on screen — copy it.
    3. On your phone, open the **WhatsApp Business App**. You'll receive a message from **Facebook Business Account** with a prompt to connect.
    4. If you don't see the message, go to **Menu (or Settings) → Account → Connect to Business Platform**.
    5. Choose whether to share up to **180 days** of chat history with Gallabox.

    <Warning>
      This decision **cannot be changed later**. If you want to change it, you must disconnect and reconnect — and history sharing will not be available again on reconnect.
    </Warning>

    6. Enter the verification code and tap **Connect to the Business Platform**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the app open while your data syncs">
    Chat and contact sync starts immediately after you connect, and can take several minutes depending on how much history you have and your internet speed. See [what syncs](/whatsapp/coexistence/what-changes#what-syncs-from-your-whatsapp-business-app) for exactly what is imported.

    <Warning>
      **Keep the WhatsApp Business App open and connected until the sync finishes.** Syncing can only be run once, and must complete within 24 hours of connecting. If it fails, the only way to retry is to disconnect and go through the whole flow again.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>
