> **Who can use this?**
>
> * Anyone whose customers' WhatsApp messages or button replies aren't showing in Gallabox.
> * Some checks need access to your business portfolio in Meta Business Suite.

<Info>

Your customers' messages reach Gallabox through Meta. If your messages are delivered but customers' replies, including quick reply button taps, never appear in the Inbox, work through the checks below in order.

</Info>

## Before You Start

Confirm the problem with one test:

1. Send a template to a number you control.
2. Reply to it from that phone, or tap a quick reply button.
3. Check whether the reply appears in the Gallabox **Inbox**.

If the reply appears, incoming messages are working. Check the reply settings on your template or broadcast instead. If it doesn't appear anywhere in Gallabox, continue below.

## Possible reasons

### 1. Your WhatsApp channel has a health issue

Check your channel's health in Gallabox:

1. Go to **WhatsApp** → **Channel Settings**.
2. Check the **Channel health** icon. If there's a problem, it shows an exclamation icon with **Fix Channel Issue**.
3. Click **Fix Channel Issue**.

If the issue is still shown after this, contact Gallabox support with the affected WhatsApp number.

### 2. Gallabox was removed as a partner

Gallabox needs partner access to your WhatsApp account in Meta. If someone in your business removed that access, Gallabox stops receiving your messages.

To check:

1. Open Meta Business Suite and click **Settings**.
2. Click **Accounts**, then select **WhatsApp accounts**.
3. Select your WhatsApp account and check that Gallabox is listed as a partner.

If Gallabox isn't listed, contact Gallabox support to restore access.

### 3. Conversation routing sends replies to another integration

If your WhatsApp number is connected to more than one integration, such as another provider or a Meta solution, Meta's conversation routing decides which one receives each type of incoming message. If replies are routed to another integration, they won't reach Gallabox.

To check:

1. Open Meta Business Suite and click **Settings**.
2. Click **Accounts**, then select **WhatsApp accounts**.
3. Select your phone number and click the **Conversation routing** tab.
4. Click **Manage** next to **Message routing**.
5. Check which integration is the responder for each type of incoming message, such as replies to marketing or utility messages. Assign Gallabox where it should receive them.

### 4. Your WhatsApp account is restricted

If Meta has restricted your WhatsApp account for a policy violation, your messaging can be affected. Check for a banner in WhatsApp Manager, or open **Business Support Home** in Meta Business Suite to see any active restriction.

## Also check: is the contact blocked?

If only one customer's messages are missing, check whether that contact is blocked in Gallabox. Messages from a blocked number are discarded and don't appear in the Inbox. See [Block and Opt-Out](/contacts/block-and-opt-out#block-a-number).

## Contact support

If none of these explain it, email [support@gallabox.com](mailto:support@gallabox.com) with:

* The affected WhatsApp number
* A customer's number whose reply is missing, with country code
* The approximate time they replied
