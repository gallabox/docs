> **Who can use this?**
>
> - Gallabox users with **Broadcast access** who create or manage WhatsApp templates.
> - Roles: Owners and Admins.

<Info>
  A template's category determines whether it's free or charged inside the 24-hour service window — see [WhatsApp Pricing](/pricing-and-billing/whatsapp-pricing). Getting the category right (and understanding when Meta can change it) avoids surprise charges and rejected templates.
</Info>

## How categories are decided

You choose a category when creating a template, but Meta validates it against the actual content — your choice doesn't guarantee approval in that category.

- **Marketing** — promotional content: offers, discounts, product announcements, re-engagement. Also covers templates with **mixed content** (part transactional, part promotional) and templates whose purpose **isn't clear** from the text — both default to Marketing.
- **Utility** — must be **non-promotional**, and must also be either specific to a user's own order/account/transaction, or genuinely time-critical (like a safety alert). Both conditions have to be true, not just one.
- **Authentication** — one-time passcodes and identity verification only. Requires using an authentication template with a configured OTP button (copy-code or one-tap), and the content can't include URLs, media, or emojis.

<Warning>
  If you submit a template as **Utility** but Meta determines it's actually promotional, **it's approved as Marketing instead** — not rejected. Review your utility templates for promotional language (upsells, offers, "don't miss out") before submitting, since this changes what you're charged for it.
</Warning>

## Category can change after approval

Meta periodically re-checks already-approved templates against the guidelines, independent of your original submission:

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Approved as Utility, but content reads as Marketing | Category is updated to Marketing. A short advance notice is given by email before the change (this notice is skipped for accounts already warned for repeated miscategorization — see below). The template keeps working, just at Marketing rates. |
| Approved as Marketing or Utility, but content should be Authentication | The template is **rejected**, not recategorized — you'll need to create a proper authentication template instead. |

You're notified by email whenever a template's category is scheduled to change or has changed.

## Requesting a review

You can request Meta review a template's category if it's:

- Categorized **Utility** or **Marketing** and currently **Rejected**, or
- Categorized **Marketing** and currently **Approved** (i.e. you believe it should actually be Utility)

If the review is approved, the category reverts; if not, it stays as Meta assessed it. You have 60 days from the category change to request a review.

## Repeated miscategorization has consequences

If Meta detects a pattern of submitting promotional templates as Utility, enforcement escalates:

| Level | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Warning** | Written notice to account admins. From here on, Utility → Marketing changes happen instantly, with no advance notice. |
| **Rate limiting** | Utility message volume is capped for a period; messages over the cap are rejected. Marketing and Authentication are unaffected. |
| **Utility restriction** | All approved Utility templates are recategorized to Marketing, and new Utility templates can't be created, for a set period. |
| **Portfolio-wide restriction** | If it continues across multiple WhatsApp numbers under the same business, the same restriction applies everywhere, for longer. |

Repeat violations after a restriction lifts can trigger longer or stricter enforcement.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I submitted a template as Utility and it got approved as Marketing — why wasn't it just rejected?">
    Meta approves templates in the category it determines is correct, rather than rejecting a mismatch outright, when the mismatch is Utility-vs-Marketing. You can use the template as-is (billed at Marketing rates), or edit the content to remove promotional language and resubmit for review.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My approved Utility template just changed to Marketing on its own — is that a bug?">
    No — Meta periodically re-reviews approved templates. If a Utility template's content reads as promotional, its category is updated, and you're notified by email. Check the template's wording against the Utility guidelines above, or request a review if you disagree.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get a template's category changed back after a review is denied?">
    No — if Meta's review upholds the new category, it stays. You can edit the template's content to more clearly match your intended category and resubmit as a new review.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why was my template rejected instead of recategorized?">
    Rejection instead of recategorization only happens when a Marketing or Utility template should actually be Authentication — Meta doesn't auto-convert those, since Authentication templates have stricter content rules (no URLs, media, or emojis, and a required OTP button). Create a proper authentication template instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
