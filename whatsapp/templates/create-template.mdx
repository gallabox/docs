This guide walks you through everything in the template editor — from creating your first template to finding its ID for API use.

<Info>

Templates live under **WhatsApp → Templates** in the left navigation. That is the only place in Gallabox where templates are created, edited, tested, and deleted. Broadcasts and sequences only pick from the templates you have already had approved here.

</Info>

<Warning>

Before submitting a Utility template, use [Smart check](/whatsapp/templates/create-template#smart-check-for-utility-templates) to review its category and content, then verify its [Configurations](/whatsapp/templates/create-template#reply-configuration) once it is approved.

</Warning>

## Plan Availability

Available on all Gallabox plans.

> **Note:** Template messages are sent at standard WhatsApp Business API rates, separate from your plan subscription.

## Before You Start

* **Owner or Admin role in Gallabox (or a role with template management permissions)** — template creation and management require these permissions.
* **An approved WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) connected to Gallabox** — templates are submitted to Meta through your connected WABA. If your channel is not connected yet, see [Connect your WhatsApp channel](/whatsapp/connect-your-whatsapp).
* **A clear message purpose.** Meta checks that the type matches the copy. Pick the type first: Custom marketing, Carousel, Limited Time Offer, Product marketing, Custom utility, Payment utility, or Authentication.

## Managing Your Templates

Go to **WhatsApp → Templates** in the left navigation. The page opens on **My Templates**, which lists every template for the WhatsApp channel selected in the channel picker at the top right.

The page has two tabs:

* **My Templates** — the templates you have created or synced from Meta.
* **Library** — ready-made templates you can open and submit as your own.

<Info>

Templates belong to a channel. If a template you expect is missing, switch the channel in the picker before assuming it wasn't created.

</Info>

### Filtering Templates

Click **Filter** above the templates list to filter by:

* **Status** — Approved, Pending, Rejected, Error
* **Category** — Marketing, Utility, Authentication
* **WABA presence** — Present in WABA, Not present in WABA

You can combine filters, and **Reset** clears them. The **Filter** button stays highlighted while any filter is active. The search box next to it searches templates by name.

### List View vs Grid View

Switch views with the **List** and **Grid** buttons at the top left of the templates list:

* **Grid** — card layout with a rendered preview of each template body. Use this to visually scan and spot formatting issues.
* **List** — table layout with name, message, language, created on, type, and status. Use this for bulk scanning.

### Syncing Templates from Meta

Meta notifies Gallabox whenever a template's status changes, so approvals and rejections normally appear on their own within minutes — you don't have to sync.

If a template shows "Approved" in Meta's WhatsApp Manager but is missing or still shows an older status in Gallabox, click the **refresh** icon at the top of the Templates page. Gallabox pulls the current list of templates from Meta for the channel selected in the picker and shows a confirmation when the sync finishes.

<Info>

Sync is per channel. If a template is still missing after a sync, check that the right channel is selected and that no filter is hiding it. If it's still missing, contact Gallabox support with the template name.

</Info>

## Creating a Template

### Step 1: Open the template editor

1. Go to **WhatsApp → Templates**.
2. Click **Create New Template**.

### Step 2: Choose a category

Select one option from the **Category** dropdown. The options are grouped by category:

| Group | Option | When to use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Marketing template** | Custom marketing | Offers and announcements with media and buttons |
| **Marketing template** | Product marketing | Catalogue or a curated product set. See [Product marketing](/whatsapp/templates/product-marketing) |
| **Marketing template** | Carousel | Up to 10 image or video cards. See [Carousel](/whatsapp/templates/carousel-templates) |
| **Marketing template** | Limited Time Offer | A code with a countdown. See [Limited Time Offer](/whatsapp/templates/limited-time-offer) |
| **Utility template** | Custom utility | Order updates and reminders tied to something the customer already did |
| **Utility template** | Payment utility | A payment request. Needs a gateway. See [WhatsApp Payments](/whatsapp/payments) |
| **Other template** | Authentication | OTPs and verification codes |

This page is the editor for **Custom marketing**, **Custom utility**, and **Authentication**. The other types have their own screens and their own docs.

Product marketing needs a connected catalogue on a Cloud API channel. Payment utility needs payments set up on that channel.

For which type to pick, and how Meta reviews marketing templates, see the [Templates overview](/whatsapp/templates/overview).

<Warning>

Category determines Meta's review criteria and per-message pricing. Submitting a promotional message as "Utility" is the single most common cause of rejections. Use [Smart check](/whatsapp/templates/create-template#smart-check-for-utility-templates) before submitting Utility templates.

</Warning>

### Step 3: Name your template

Enter a **Template name**. Gallabox formats what you type as you type it: letters become lowercase and anything that isn't a letter or number becomes an underscore. Maximum 60 characters. Example: `order_confirmation_en`

This name is how the template is referenced in the Gallabox API and in broadcast and sequence flows. It cannot be changed after submission.

### Step 4: Select the channel and language

Choose the **Channel** this template belongs to and the **Language** it is written in. If you need the same template in another language, duplicate it and change the language on the copy (see [Cloning a Template](/whatsapp/templates/create-template#cloning-a-template)).

### Step 5: Write the message body

Enter your message text in **Message body**.

* Maximum 1,024 characters. The editor shows the count and warns you when you go over.
* Click **Add variables** to insert a contact variable (`{{name}}`, `{{email}}`, `{{phone}}`) or **Add Custom** to create your own placeholder.
* Every variable needs a sample value — Meta's review team uses it to understand the message.

See [Variables](/whatsapp/templates/create-template#variables) below for the details.

#### Rewriting your message with AI

If you'd rather not draft the body from scratch, click **AI Rewrite** above the message body.

The panel shows your draft under **Your content** and the suggestion under **AI content**, side by side, so you can compare before committing. From there:

* **Apply AI content** — replace your draft with the suggestion.
* **Rewrite again** — generate a different suggestion from the same draft.
* **Apply original** — discard the suggestion and keep what you wrote.

Nothing changes until you click one of the Apply buttons. Edit the AI content directly in the panel first if it's close but not right.

<Note>
AI Rewrite draws on your AI credits, and the panel shows your remaining usage. See [AI Wallet](/pricing-and-billing/ai-wallet).
</Note>

<Warning>
AI Rewrite improves how a message reads — it doesn't check it against Meta's template policy. A rewritten Utility template can drift into promotional language and get rejected or recategorised, so run [Smart check](/whatsapp/templates/create-template#smart-check-for-utility-templates) after rewriting, not before.
</Warning>

### Step 6: Add a header (optional)

The **Message header** appears above the body text. Choose one type:

* **None** — no header
* **Text** — up to 60 characters, can include one variable
* **Image** — upload a sample image
* **Video** — upload a sample video
* **Document** — upload a sample document
* **Location** — the message carries a location, filled in when you send

### Step 7: Add a footer (optional)

The **Footer** appears below the body text. Maximum 60 characters. No variables.

When you pick **Custom marketing**, Gallabox pre-fills: *Reply with 'STOP' to unsubscribe from marketing messages.* You can change the wording. Keep a STOP line. Meta rejects marketing templates with no way to opt out.

Carousel and Limited Time Offer have no footer field. That is a Meta rule, not a Gallabox setting.

### Step 8: Add buttons (optional)

Click **Add a button** to add buttons to the template. See [Button Types](/whatsapp/templates/create-template#button-types) below.

### Step 9: Submit for approval

Click **Submit for approval**. Gallabox sends the template to Meta for review, and the template appears in your list with status **Pending**.

Meta usually returns a decision within 24 hours. If a template stays Pending for longer than that, raise a support ticket with Gallabox.

<Info>

After a template is approved, wait about 15 minutes before using it. Meta needs time to propagate the approval, and messages sent immediately can fail.

</Info>

## Button Types

| Button type             | What it does                                                 | Max per template | When to use                                    |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Quick reply**         | Sends a predefined text reply when the customer taps         | 10               | Reply options, opt-out, multi-choice responses |
| **URL**                 | Opens a link in the customer's browser                       | 2                | Product pages, booking links, order tracking   |
| **Call phone number**   | Opens the customer's phone dialler with your number          | 1                | Customer service, callback requests            |
| **Coupon code**         | Copies a promo code to the customer's clipboard              | 1                | Promotions, voucher codes                      |
| **Form**                | Opens a WhatsApp form you have built in Gallabox             | 1                | Lead capture, structured data collection       |
| **Call on WhatsApp**    | Starts a WhatsApp call to your business                      | 1                | Channels with WhatsApp calling enabled         |

**Button limit:** Up to 10 buttons per template in any combination. Button text is limited to 25 characters. If you add more than three buttons, WhatsApp shows them in a list instead of as separate buttons.

### Coupon code

Add **Coupon code** when the customer should copy a promo code. You get one per template.

* The button label is **Copy code**. You cannot change it.
* **Sample code** is what Meta reviews. Maximum 15 characters. Example: `GET50OFF`.
* Fill the live code when you send the template (broadcast, sequence, inbox, or API).
* Copy code does not work on WhatsApp Web or desktop. The customer needs the phone app.

A coupon on a Custom marketing template is not the same as a [Limited Time Offer](/whatsapp/templates/limited-time-offer). Use Limited Time Offer when you also need a countdown.

### Form

**Form** opens a WhatsApp form (a Flow) inside the chat. You can add one.

The form must already be published under [Forms](/whatsapp/flows). Pick it in **Select form**, set the button text, and optionally pick an icon: Default, Document, Promotion, or Review.

### Track link clicks

On URL buttons, turn on **Track link clicks** to record taps in broadcast analytics and message trackers. The customer still lands on your URL. Gallabox wraps it so the tap is counted.

## Variables

Variables are placeholders replaced with real values when the template is sent. Gallabox uses **named** variables, not numbered ones.

### Contact variables

Click **Add variables** in the message body and pick one from the **Contact** group:

| Variable      | What it inserts        |
| ------------- | ---------------------- |
| `{{name}}`    | Contact's name         |
| `{{email}}`   | Contact's email address |
| `{{phone}}`   | Contact's phone number |

These are filled automatically from the contact record when the message is sent. Email and phone are hidden from users whose role cannot view personal information.

### Custom variables

Click **Add variables → Add Custom** to insert a placeholder such as `{{variable_1}}`, then rename it to something meaningful — for example `{{order_id}}`. You (or your broadcast, sequence, or API call) supply the value at send time.

Example body: `Hi {{name}}, your order {{order_id}} has been dispatched. Estimated delivery: {{delivery_date}}.`

Rules:

* Give every variable a sample value before submitting. Meta requires samples to review the template.
* Variable names are limited to 50 characters.
* A variable at the very start of the body, with no text before it, is a common rejection reason.

## Smart Check for Utility Templates

**Smart check** is an AI-powered pre-submission check for **Utility templates only**. It analyses your template body and tells you how likely Meta is to approve it as a Utility template — before you submit.

### How to run Smart check

1. Write your Utility template body in the editor.
2. Click **Smart check** at the top of the editor (visible only when a Utility category is selected).
3. Gallabox runs the check and returns a confidence score:

| Confidence | Score | What it means                                | What to do                                         |
| ---------- | ----- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **High**   | > 80  | Template reads as a genuine Utility message  | Safe to submit                                     |
| **Medium** | 26–80 | Some risk of rejection                       | Review the reasoning; use the suggested rewrite    |
| **Low**    | ≤ 25  | Template likely reads as a Marketing message | Rework the content or change category to Marketing |

4. When the score is Medium, Gallabox also shows:
   * **Reasoning** — why the template may be misclassified
   * **Suggested rewrite** — an alternative version you can copy into the editor

<Warning>

Smart check is a pre-submission guide, not a guarantee. Meta's review is independent. A high Smart check score reduces rejection risk but does not guarantee approval.

</Warning>

## Cloning a Template

Approved templates are read-only in Gallabox — the editor opens them marked **View Only**. To change an approved template, duplicate it and submit the copy. Duplicating is also how you create language variants, A/B tests, and fixed versions of rejected templates.

**From the templates list:**

1. In **WhatsApp → Templates**, find the template you want to clone.
2. Click the **three-dot menu** on the template card (Grid) or row (List).
3. Click **Duplicate template**. For a rejected or errored template, the same action reads **Edit template**.

**From the template itself:** open the template and click **Duplicate this template** (or **Edit template** if it was rejected or errored).

The editor opens with all content pre-filled. Change the **Template name** — names must be unique — make your edits, and click **Submit**. The original template is unaffected, and both exist independently in your list.

## Finding the Template ID (for API use)

Every template has a unique Template ID used when sending via the Gallabox API or when referencing templates in external systems.

**Method 1 — Three-dot menu:**

1. Go to **WhatsApp → Templates**.
2. Open the **three-dot menu** on the template, in the list or on the template itself.
3. Click **Copy template ID**. On an approved template, **Copy as API** copies a ready-made API payload instead.

**Method 2 — URL:** When you open a template, the ID appears in the browser URL: `.../whatsapp-template-messagesv2/template/{template_id}`

Copy the value after the last `/`.

## Testing a Template

Before using an approved template in a broadcast or automation, send a test message to verify the content and variable fill-in look correct on a real device.

<Warning>

Test messages are sent as real WhatsApp messages. Standard WhatsApp API message rates apply — this costs real message credits.

</Warning>

1. Go to **WhatsApp → Templates**.
2. Open an **Approved** template, or use its three-dot menu. (**Test template** is only available for approved templates.)
3. Click **Test template**.
4. Enter the **phone number** to send the test to, with its country code.
5. Fill in the values for each variable in the preview.
6. Click **Test**.

Check the receiving device to verify formatting, variable substitution, and button display. From the confirmation you can **Go to conversation** to see the message in the inbox, or **Test with another number**.

## Reply Configuration

**Configurations** sets up what happens when a customer replies to a specific template — either by tapping a **Quick reply** button or sending a free-text message. This is configured per template, and it is available once the template is approved.

1. Go to **WhatsApp → Templates**.
2. Open the **three-dot menu** on the template and click **Configurations**.
3. Under **Reply Settings → Assign to**, choose a **team**, **user**, or **AI agent or bot**.
4. Optionally tick **Override the current assignments?** to reassign conversations that already have an owner.
5. Save the configuration.

Clearing the assignment and saving removes the configuration. On approved templates, the same **Configurations** dialog also holds the message tracker settings for that template.

Reply settings are available on all Gallabox plans.

<Info>

Configurations only apply to replies to that specific template message. Replies to other messages in the same conversation follow your standard inbox routing rules.

</Info>

## Common Errors

| Error or symptom                                                 | Why it happens                                                                                       | How to fix it                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **"Variable in wrong position"** rejection                       | A variable appears at the very start of the body text                                                | Add at least one word before the first variable, e.g. "Hi \{\{name\}\},"                                                      |
| **Template stuck in Pending**                                    | Meta's review queue is slower than usual                                                             | Meta usually decides within 24 hours. If a template is Pending for longer, raise a support ticket with Gallabox.             |
| **Smart check shows Low confidence but the template is Utility** | The body contains offer language ("save", "discount", "exclusive") without a clear transaction context | Run the suggested rewrite or remove promotional language — tie the message to a specific transaction                          |
| **Duplicate template name error**                                | A template with that name already exists in your WABA — or you deleted an approved template with that name in the last 30 days | Choose a unique, descriptive name, e.g. `order_confirm_v2_en`, or wait out the 30-day window                                 |
| **Message fails right after approval**                           | The approval has not propagated to Meta's servers yet                                                 | Wait about 15 minutes after approval before sending with a new template                                                      |
| **Test message not received**                                    | Phone number format error or message filtered by the receiving device                                | Confirm the country code is included. Test with a different phone.                                                            |
| **Approved in Meta but missing in Gallabox**                     | The status update hasn't arrived, or you're looking at a different channel                           | Select the right channel, clear filters, and click the refresh icon. If still missing, contact Gallabox support with the name. |
| **Template can't be deleted**                                    | Meta does not allow deleting a template that it has disabled                                         | Leave the disabled template in place and create a replacement under a new name                                                |

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Where do I create templates — Broadcasts or WhatsApp?">
**WhatsApp → Templates** in the left navigation. Broadcasts and sequences only select from templates that are already approved; they cannot create or edit one. If a template you need isn't in a broadcast's picker, go to **WhatsApp → Templates** and check its status.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I submitted my template and it's still Pending — is something broken?">
Not necessarily. Meta's review typically takes 24–48 hours, but new WABA accounts or accounts with recent quality flags take longer. Check **WhatsApp → Templates** for the current status. If it shows Pending after 10 days, contact Gallabox support with your template name and submission date.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I edit an approved template?">
Not in Gallabox. An approved template opens as **View Only**. Click **Duplicate this template**, edit the copy, and submit it for approval under a new name. The original stays active and usable while the copy is in review. Rejected and errored templates can be edited the same way, through **Edit template**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My template was approved but it's sending with the wrong variable values — what do I do?">
The template itself is correct — the issue is in how variable values are passed at send time. Check the broadcast, sequence, or API call that's sending the template and verify the variable mapping. In Gallabox broadcasts, go back to the broadcast setup and check the column mapping for each variable.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why does Smart check say Low confidence on my Utility template? It's definitely transactional.">
Smart check looks for language patterns Meta uses to classify templates. Words like "offer", "exclusive", "limited time", or "discount" trigger Marketing classification even in transactional contexts. Look at the reasoning Smart check returns — it identifies the specific phrases causing the low score. Replace those phrases or remove them entirely.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I deleted a template — can I reuse its name?">
Not immediately, if it was approved. Meta blocks the name of a deleted **approved** template for 30 days. Rejected and errored templates don't hold their names. If you don't want to wait, create the new template with a variation on the name, such as `order_confirm_v2_en`.

Two other Meta rules are worth knowing: a template you delete while a message using it is still undelivered goes to **Pending deletion** while WhatsApp keeps trying to deliver it for 30 days, and templates Meta has **disabled** cannot be deleted at all.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A template I created is missing from the list.">
Templates are listed per channel. Switch the WhatsApp channel in the picker at the top right of the Templates page, clear any active filters, and click the refresh icon to sync the latest status from Meta.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
