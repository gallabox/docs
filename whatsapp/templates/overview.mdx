> **Who can use this?**
>
> * Owners, Admins, and roles with WhatsApp Templates access.
> * A connected WhatsApp channel is required before the Templates page can be used.

## What are template messages

You cannot start a WhatsApp conversation with a free-typed message. Meta only lets you reach out with a template it has already approved. Gallabox submits that template for you. Meta reviews it.

Gallabox does not have one "marketing template." **Create New Template** shows four marketing types, plus utility and authentication. Pick the type that matches the job, not the category name on Meta's website.

<Info>

Template messages are charged per send by category. See [Message Credits](/pricing-and-billing/message-credits).

Replies inside the 24-hour window are free-form. Those do not use templates.

</Info>

Available on all Gallabox plans.

## Pick a type

Go to **WhatsApp → Templates**, then click **Create New Template**. The **Category** dropdown is grouped the same way as the table below.

### Marketing

Use these when you are promoting something. Meta bills them as Marketing.

<Columns cols="2">
  <Card title="Custom marketing" href="/whatsapp/templates/create-template">
    Offers, announcements, and re-engagement. Add media, a coupon, a website button, or a form.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Carousel" href="/whatsapp/templates/carousel-templates">
    One message, then up to 10 image or video cards the customer swipes through.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Limited Time Offer" href="/whatsapp/templates/limited-time-offer">
    A code with a countdown on the customer's phone. Built for flash sales.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Product marketing" href="/whatsapp/templates/product-marketing">
    Open your full catalogue, or send a curated set of products from Commerce Manager.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Marketing templates need an opt-out. Gallabox pre-fills the footer with *Reply with 'STOP' to unsubscribe from marketing messages.* Keep that line, or write your own STOP wording. Carousel and Limited Time Offer do not have a footer. Meta still expects the message itself to be honest marketing, not spam.

### Utility and authentication

| Type in Gallabox | When to use | Where to set it up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Custom utility** | Order updates, appointment reminders, delivery notices. Tied to something the customer already did. | [Create a template](/whatsapp/templates/create-template) |
| **Payment utility** | Ask the customer to pay in WhatsApp. Needs a payment gateway on that channel. | Create it here, then send it from [WhatsApp Payments](/whatsapp/payments) |
| **Authentication** | OTPs and verification codes. No offers, no extra copy. | [Create a template](/whatsapp/templates/create-template). **Copy code** is the option most teams use. |

Do not put a discount in a Utility template. Meta will reject it, or recategorise it as Marketing and bill it that way. For Utility drafts, run [Smart check](/whatsapp/templates/create-template#smart-check-for-utility-templates) before you submit.

### Authentication templates

Authentication is only for codes. Pick **Authentication template** in the Category dropdown. **Copy code** works on phone and WhatsApp Web. **One-tap Autofill** and **Zero-tap Autofill** need an Android app and extra setup. Do not add offers or extra marketing copy. Meta will reject it.

## How Meta treats marketing templates

Approval is not the finish line.

**Quality rating** shows on the template as High, Medium, or Low. Low quality can pause the template, disable it, or make later sends fail. If Gallabox warns that a template is Low quality, stop using it and submit a clearer version.

**Volume.** Meta can limit how many marketing messages one person receives. If a broadcast delivers to some contacts and fails for others, check quality first, then whether those people already got a lot of marketing messages from you.

**Paused** and **Disabled** are Meta decisions. You cannot turn those back on from Gallabox. Duplicate the template, fix the copy, and submit a new one.

## The approval process

<Steps>
<Step title="Create the template">
Go to **WhatsApp → Templates** and click **Create New Template**. Pick a type, write the message, add sample values for every variable.
</Step>
<Step title="Submit to Meta">
Click **Submit for approval**. Gallabox sends it to Meta.
</Step>
<Step title="Wait for a decision">
A decision usually comes within 24 hours. If it takes longer, raise a support ticket with Gallabox.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the status in Gallabox">
* **Pending:** Meta is reviewing it
* **Approved:** wait about 15 minutes, then you can send it
* **Rejected:** Meta gives a reason. Duplicate, fix, resubmit
* **Error:** it never reached Meta. The template shows why
* **Paused** or **Disabled:** Meta stopped it after people marked it as spam or low quality
</Step>
</Steps>

<Info>

Approvals usually land in Gallabox on their own. If Meta shows Approved and Gallabox does not, pick the right channel on the Templates page and click the refresh icon.

</Info>

### Why templates get rejected

* **Wrong category.** An offer submitted as Utility is the most common miss.
* **Missing opt-out.** Custom marketing with no STOP line in the footer.
* **Vague copy.** The message does not say what it is for. Meta cannot guess from the template name.
* **Missing samples.** Every variable needs an example value.
* **Brand mismatch.** The copy does not match your verified WhatsApp business name.

## Template limits

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Active templates | Based on your WhatsApp quality rating |
| Template name | 60 characters, lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores |
| Message body | 1,024 characters |
| Header / footer | 60 characters each (Limited Time Offer's offer line is 16) |
| Buttons | Up to 10. Button text up to 25 characters |

Carousel, Limited Time Offer, and Product marketing have extra rules on their own pages.

## How to create a template

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Templates">
Go to **WhatsApp → Templates**.
</Step>
<Step title="Create New Template">
Click **Create New Template**.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the type">
Use **Category**. Match the job: Custom marketing, Carousel, Limited Time Offer, Product marketing, Custom utility, Payment utility, or Authentication.
</Step>
<Step title="Fill the editor">
Follow the page for that type. Custom marketing and Custom utility stay on [Create and manage templates](/whatsapp/templates/create-template).
</Step>
<Step title="Submit for approval">
Click **Submit for approval**, then wait for **Approved**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Check>

Start with Custom utility for real orders and appointments. Those approve faster. Add marketing types once the utility library works.

</Check>

## Example

You run a restaurant. You need three jobs, so you create three templates.

1. **Custom utility, order confirmation**
   > Hi \{\{name\}\}! We have received your order \{\{order_id\}\}. Estimated delivery: \{\{delivery_time\}\}.
2. **Custom utility, out for delivery**
   > Your order \{\{order_id\}\} is on the way. Your rider is \{\{partner_name\}\}.
3. **Custom marketing, weekly offer**
   > Hi \{\{name\}\}! This week: \{\{discount\}\}% off \{\{product\}\}. Use code \{\{code\}\} at checkout.
   Keep the STOP footer Gallabox adds.

A flash sale with a countdown is a [Limited Time Offer](/whatsapp/templates/limited-time-offer), not Custom marketing with a date in the body.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Why was my template rejected?">
Open the template. Meta's reason is in the details.

* **"Too promotional":** move it to a Marketing type, or strip offer language if it is meant to be Utility.
* **"Missing opt-out":** keep a STOP line in the footer on Custom marketing.
* **"Vague purpose":** say what the message is in the body. Do not hide it in the name.
* **Variables:** add a sample for each one, and put text before the first variable.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I edit an approved template?">
Not in place. Approved templates open as **View Only**. Click **Duplicate this template**, edit the copy, and submit it under a new name. The original stays usable while Meta reviews the copy.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What's the difference between a template and a free-form message?">
A free-form message is anything you type in the inbox. You can only send it within 24 hours of the customer's last message. A template is pre-approved, so you can send it any time, including in broadcasts and sequences.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I use variables?">
In the editor, click **Add variables**. Contact fields such as `{{name}}` fill from the contact. Custom ones such as `{{order_id}}` are filled when you send. Give every variable a sample before you submit. See [Create and manage templates](/whatsapp/templates/create-template#variables).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
