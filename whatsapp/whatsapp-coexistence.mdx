## Plan Availability

Available on all Gallabox plans.

## What is Co-existence?

Co-existence (also called **Embedded Signup**) lets you use the **WhatsApp Business App** on your phone **and** Gallabox on the **same phone number** at the same time.

- **WhatsApp Business App** — continue personal, 1:1 conversations from your phone
- **Gallabox** — run shared inbox, automation, campaigns, and AI agents for your team

Both work on the same phone number. Messages are synced between both.

<Info>
  Message status like Delivered, Read are not syncing from the Whatsapp Business App in real time for Co-Existence channels.This is a known limitation from Meta.
</Info>

## Why Use Co-existence?

Before Co-existence, businesses had to choose:

- **WhatsApp Business App** — simple and free, but limited for teams and automation
- **WhatsApp Business API** — powerful, but conversations moved to the platform's inbox

Co-existence removes this trade-off — the app and Gallabox stay in sync on the same number. It's best suited for:

- **Founder-led sales** — you handle high-touch deals from the app; your team manages follow-ups and bulk campaigns in Gallabox
- **Support teams scaling up** — support moves into Gallabox with assignments, SLAs, and AI agents, while you can still respond from your phone when needed
- **Gradual migration** — test Cloud API features first, then decide if you want to go fully API-first later

## How Co-existence Works

When you enable Co-existence:

1. Your team gets a **shared inbox** in Gallabox — with assignments, tags, bot flows, broadcasts, and AI agents
2. You (and your team members) can still reply from the **WhatsApp Business App** on your phones
3. **1:1 chat history** (up to 6 months) can be synced into Gallabox if you choose
4. New messages are **mirrored between both** — what you send from the app appears in Gallabox, and vice versa

<Tip>
  If you require high volume of messages, one channel to advertise, automation requirements at scale you should consider moving from Business App to Whatsapp Cloud API for these capabilities.
</Tip>

### What changes on the WhatsApp Business App

After Co-existence is enabled, some features are **no longer available** on the app:

| Feature | What happens after Co-existence |
| --- | --- |
| Edit messages | No longer supported |
| Delete/revoke messages | No longer supported |
| Disappearing messages | Automatically turned off for all 1:1 chats |
| View once media | Disabled for all 1:1 chats |
| Live location sharing | Disabled for all 1:1 chats |
| Broadcast lists | Cannot create new lists; existing lists become read-only |
| Group chats | Not synced to Gallabox — stay on the phone only |
| Voice and video calls | Not available via Cloud API |
| Catalog, orders, WhatsApp Status | No change — these stay on the app |

### What you can do in Gallabox after Co-existence

|  | From Gallabox |
| --- | --- |
| Shared inbox | All team members see conversations |
| Automation | Bot flows, AI agents, sequences, broadcasts |
| Routing | Auto-assign conversations to teams or agents |
| Campaigns | Send broadcast campaigns (WhatsApp API pricing applies) |
| Analytics | Full conversation and campaign reporting |

### What stays the same on the WhatsApp Business App

- 1:1 conversations continue as normal on the phone
- WhatsApp Business App features (catalog, quick replies, labels, Status) remain in the app
- Group chats stay on the phone only

## Requirements

Before enabling Co-existence, make sure you have:

1. **WhatsApp Business App** version **2.24.17 or higher** installed on your phone
2. A phone number from a **supported country** (Co-existence is not available for Nigeria or South Africa)
3. The number **not already connected to another WhatsApp API provider** — if it is, that connection must be disconnected first
4. **Gallabox Co-existence enabled** for your workspace (contact support)

<Info>
  You can plan a migration of a number from Co-Existence to a Cloud channel, but it is impossible to move a Cloud Channel to Co-Existence. These limitations are important.
</Info>

## How to Set Up

Co-existence is set up in four stages. Make sure support has enabled Co-existence for your workspace before starting.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact Gallabox support">
    Email [support@gallabox.com](mailto:support@gallabox.com) with:

    - Your WhatsApp Business App phone number
    - A note that you want to enable Co-existence

    Gallabox will verify your eligibility and activate the feature on your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Eligibility check">
    The Gallabox team will confirm:

    - Your number is on the WhatsApp Business App (not already on Cloud API)
    - Your country is supported
    - No existing credit-line or partner conflicts on your number
  </Step>
  <Step title="Embedded Signup">
    Once eligible, complete the onboarding through Gallabox:

    1. Start the connection flow in Gallabox and choose to connect your existing WhatsApp Business App number.
    2. A **verification code** will appear on screen — copy it.
    3. On your phone, open the **WhatsApp Business App**. You'll receive a message from **Facebook Business Account** with a prompt to connect.
    4. If you don't see the message, go to **Menu (or Settings) → Account → Connect to Business Platform**.
    5. Choose whether to share up to **6 months of chat history** with Gallabox.

    <Warning>
      This decision **cannot be changed later**. If you want to change it, you must disconnect and reconnect — and history sharing will not be available again on reconnect.
    </Warning>

    6. Enter the verification code and tap **Connect to the Business Platform**.

    <Info>
      Chat history sync can take a few minutes depending on your message history size and internet speed. Keep the WhatsApp Business App open during this time.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start using Co-existence">
    Once connected:

    - Chats and contacts begin syncing into Gallabox
    - Your team can work from the Gallabox shared inbox
    - You can continue replying from the WhatsApp Business App
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Linked Devices After Setup

After connecting, all **linked companion devices** (WhatsApp Web, WhatsApp on tablet, etc.) will be **automatically unlinked**. This is a Meta security requirement during Cloud API onboarding.

You can re-link them after setup is complete.

## Keeping Co-existence Active

The connection between your WhatsApp Business App and Gallabox must stay active. Meta can automatically remove Gallabox from your WhatsApp Business Account in these situations:

| Reason | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Number offline for 14\+ days** | Your WhatsApp Business App is inactive or unreachable — Meta severs the connection automatically |
| **Device change or app reinstall** | Switching phones, resetting, or reinstalling the app can break the connection |
| **Compliance or integrity issues** | Policy violations or low trust score on your account |

<Warning>
  If Gallabox stops receiving messages or your shared inbox goes silent, your Co-existence connection may have been broken. [Contact Gallabox support](mailto:support@gallabox.com) to confirm and re-onboard.
</Warning>

### Best practices to avoid unexpected disconnections

1. **Keep the WhatsApp Business App active** — open and use it regularly. Avoid leaving the number dormant for more than a few days.
2. **Avoid frequent device changes** — if you need to change phones, contact Gallabox support first.
3. **Stay compliant with Meta's policies** — messaging practices, business profile, and account standing all affect the connection.

## How to Disconnect

To stop using Co-existence:

1. Open the **WhatsApp Business App** on your phone.
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Business Platform → Disconnect Account**.
3. Confirm disconnection.

This removes Gallabox from your WhatsApp Business Account. Your WhatsApp Business App continues working normally.

If you need help disconnecting, [contact Gallabox support](mailto:support@gallabox.com).

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will I lose my existing WhatsApp chats?">
    No — if you approved history sharing during onboarding, up to 6 months of recent 1:1 chats are synced into Gallabox. Chats older than 6 months are not synced. If you did not approve history sharing, no chats are imported.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I still reply from my phone?">
    Yes. The WhatsApp Business App continues to work normally for 1:1 conversations. You can reply from the app or from Gallabox — both are synced.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are group chats available in Gallabox?">
    No. Group chats remain only in the WhatsApp Business App and are not synced to Gallabox.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the WhatsApp Business App removed?">
    No. It continues to work alongside Gallabox. You have both — the app on your phone and the shared inbox in Gallabox.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are messages free?">
    Messages sent from the **WhatsApp Business App** are free. Messages sent through **Gallabox** follow WhatsApp Cloud API per-message pricing. See [message credits](/pricing-and-billing/message-credits) for rates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if my number was previously connected to another API provider?">
    Existing credit-line or partner connections can block Co-existence setup. Gallabox support will help resolve this during the eligibility check.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why are my linked devices disconnected?">
    Meta automatically unlinks companion devices (WhatsApp Web, tablet, etc.) during Cloud API onboarding for security and compatibility. Re-link them after setup is complete.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
