> **Who can use this?**
>
> * Anyone whose WhatsApp Business Account Meta has restricted for a policy violation.
> * You need access to your business portfolio in Meta Business Suite.

<Info>

When a WhatsApp Business Account breaks a WhatsApp policy, Meta can restrict, lock, or disable it. If you believe your business follows WhatsApp's policies, you can ask Meta to review the decision. This is called an appeal. Meta decides every appeal, so it's submitted from your Meta account, not from Gallabox.

</Info>

## What a restriction looks like

Meta usually starts with a warning. Repeated violations lead to restrictions that get longer each time:

| Restriction | What it blocks |
| :-- | :-- |
| 1 or 3 day block | Sending marketing, utility, and authentication templates, and adding phone numbers |
| 5, 7, or 30 day block | Sending any messages, and adding phone numbers |
| Account lock | Sending any messages, with no end date. Only an appeal can remove it. |
| Disabled | The account is removed from the WhatsApp Business Platform. |

For severe violations, such as scams or the sale of illegal drugs, Meta can disable an account straight away.

Meta tells you about a violation by email to your business portfolio's admins, with a banner in WhatsApp Manager, and in the notifications center in Meta Business Suite. In Gallabox, failed messages show error `368` or `131031`. See [Message Error Codes](/whatsapp/message-error-codes).

## Before You Start

* **Read the violation first.** Business Support Home shows which policy Meta says you broke, with examples of what's allowed. Make sure you understand it before you appeal.
* **Not every violation can be appealed.** Some spam restrictions can't be reviewed. If yours can't, you'll need to wait until the restriction ends.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Business Support Home">
Open it in either of these ways:

* In Meta Business Suite, click **All tools** in the left-side menu, then click **Business Support Home**. If you don't see your accounts, click **Account Overview** (the speedometer icon).
* Go to **Business Settings** → **WhatsApp Accounts**, select your account, open **WhatsApp Manager**, then go to **Business Support Home**.
</Step>

<Step title="Select your WhatsApp account">
Select the WhatsApp account that was restricted.
</Step>

<Step title="Request a review">
Choose the violation from the list and click **Request Review**.
</Step>

<Step title="Explain your business and submit">
Enter your supporting details in the dialog, then click **Submit**. See [What to include in your appeal](#what-to-include-in-your-appeal).

Your request moves to the **In Review** tab.
</Step>

</Steps>

## What to include in your appeal

Meta reviews your appeal against the policy you were flagged for. A clear picture of your business helps the reviewer. Include:

* A brief description of your business
* When your business was started
* Where your business is located
* Why you want to use WhatsApp for your business
* How you plan to reach your customers on WhatsApp
* What kind of customers you're trying to reach

## After you submit

Meta usually decides within **24 to 48 hours** and sends the decision to your Meta Business account. The violation is then marked either:

* **Reversed:** Meta agrees with your appeal, and your account returns to its previous status.
* **Unchanged:** the violation and any restriction stay in place.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Can Gallabox remove the restriction for me?">
No. Restrictions and appeals are decided by Meta, and only Meta can reverse them. Gallabox support can help you understand the violation and what to include in your appeal.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can't find a Request Review button. Why?">
Not every violation can be appealed. Some spam restrictions can't be reviewed, and you need to wait for the restriction to end before you can send messages again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I send messages while my appeal is in review?">
No. The restriction stays in place until Meta reverses it or the restriction period ends. What you can't send depends on the restriction — see [What a restriction looks like](#what-a-restriction-looks-like).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I avoid future restrictions?">
Follow the [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy). Only message customers who've opted in, honour opt-outs, and pick the right category for each template.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
